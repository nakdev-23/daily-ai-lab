# Getting started with Suno

Suno is an AI (artificial intelligence) tool for creating music that helps everyone — whether or not they have a musical background — create high-quality songs in seconds. Just type a description, and Suno will compose the song, add the melody, and sing it for you.

---

## What is Suno?

Suno is a Generative AI Music platform that uses a Large Language Model and an Audio Generation Model working together. The result is a song that has all of:

- **Vocals** — the AI actually sings, sounding natural
- **Instrumentation** — full backing music
- **Lyrics** — generate lyrics automatically or use your own

---

## Sign Up

### Sign-up steps

1. Go to the website **[suno.com](https://suno.com)**
2. Click the **"Sign Up"** or **"Log In"** button
3. Choose a login method:
   - **Google Account**
   - **Discord Account**
   - **Microsoft Account**
   - **Apple ID**
   - **Email**

### Important notes

- Users must be at least **13 years** old to sign up
- For users under 18, parental consent is required
- If you have a problem logging in with a phone number, try another method such as Google or Discord instead

## Create Your First Song

### The easiest way — Simple Mode

1. Click the **"Create"** button on the menu bar
2. In the **"Song Description"** text box, type what you want, such as:
   - `"A fun Pop song about summer vacation"`
   - `"A cool Hip-Hop song talking about dreams"`
   - `"An R&B ballad (slow song) about love"`
3. Click the green **"Create"** button
4. Suno creates 2 versions for you, taking about 30–60 seconds

### Examples of good Prompts (song-creation commands)

```
fun, bright, Pop music, about a new morning full of energy
```

```
Sad ballad, piano and strings, the feeling of missing someone
```

```
Energetic EDM, club music, dancing all night
```

## Credits — the unit for creating songs

Suno uses a **Credits** system (the unit exchanged to create songs). Each time you create a song, it uses credits, as follows:

| Plan | Credits per month | Approximate number of songs |
|-----------|--------------|-------------------|
| **Free** | 50 credits | ~10 songs |
| **Pro** | 2,500 credits | ~500 songs |
| **Premier** | 10,000 credits | ~2,000 songs |

> Creating a song 1 time = 2 songs (2 versions) = 10 credits

## Download and share songs

After creating a song, you can:

- **Play** — listen to the song on the website
- **Download** — save as an MP3 or MP4 (video) file
- **Share** — copy the link or share to Social Media
- **Publish/Unpublish** — control who sees your song

---

## Tips for beginners

1. **Start simple** — describe the feeling or mood you want, such as "fun," "sad," "energetic"
2. **Add the Genre** — specify clearly, such as Pop, Rock, Jazz, Hip-Hop, Classical
3. **Create multiple times** — the result may differ each time; try again if not satisfied
4. **Use Custom Mode** when you want more control over the song's content
5. **Look at Explore** for inspiration from others' songs

## Frequently asked questions

**Q: Do I need to know music beforehand?**
A: Not necessarily! Suno is designed for everyone to use, even with no musical background at all.

**Q: How long are the songs created?**
A: Usually 2–4 minutes, but can be extended up to 8 minutes with the Extend feature.

**Q: Can I use it on mobile?**
A: Yes! Suno has an app for both iOS (iPhone) and Android.

**Q: Are created songs public or private?**
A: It depends on the settings; you can hide songs if you want privacy.
