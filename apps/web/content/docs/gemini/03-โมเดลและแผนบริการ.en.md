## The Gemini models available

Gemini has 3 levels of models to choose from, powered by the **Gemini 3** family (including Gemini 3 Flash and Gemini 3.1 Pro):

| Model | Name | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| **Fast** | Gemini Flash | General work, summarizing, brainstorming — the fastest |
| **Thinking** | Gemini Thinking | Complex problems needing reasoning — a balance of speed and accuracy |
| **Pro** | Gemini 3.1 Pro | Advanced math, code, analyzing large files |

### How to change the model
1. Go to [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com)
2. In the message box, click the model name shown
3. Choose the model you want

> **Tip:** Fast is free; Thinking and Pro have usage limits, which you can raise with a subscription.

## What is a Context Window?

A context window is the amount of data Gemini can "read and understand" at once. Think of it like the AI's "short-term memory."

| Size | Equivalent data |
|---|---|
| 32K tokens | About 24,000 words of text |
| 128K tokens | About 96,000 words of text |
| 1 million tokens | 1,500 pages of text or 30,000 lines of code |

If an uploaded file is larger than the context window, Gemini may not answer fully or may miss details, especially for work that analyzes a whole large document.

## Usage Limits

Gemini has usage limits so it can serve everyone fairly.

**What determines usage:**
- The length and complexity of the prompt
- The size and number of uploaded files
- The length of the conversation

**When you reach a model's limit:**
- You can switch back to **Fast** to continue in the same chat
- Wait for the limit to reset, or upgrade your plan

> **Note:** Limits may change without notice, especially during high-traffic periods.

## How to upgrade your plan

1. Go to [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com)
2. Click the menu (☰) → **Settings & help** → **Manage plan**
3. Or go to [gemini.google/subscriptions](https://gemini.google/subscriptions)

Google AI plans are part of **Google One** — usable only with a personal account.

---

## Summary

Choose the model and plan to suit your usage:
- For general work → use **Fast** free
- For analyzing large documents or doing Deep Research often → **Pro** is worth it
- For all the features including the Agent and Deep Think → **Ultra**
