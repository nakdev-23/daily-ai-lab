# 11 — Connected Apps — connect Gemini to your apps

Reference: [Use & Manage Connected Apps](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13695044) | [Google Workspace](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/15229592)

---

## What are Connected Apps

**Connected Apps** is a feature that connects Gemini to various apps and services, both Google's and third-party, so Gemini can help with work in those apps directly.

Once connected, Gemini can:
- Read and summarize emails in Gmail
- Add appointments in Google Calendar
- Create documents in Google Docs
- Find files in Google Drive
- And much more

---

## How to connect an app

1. Go to [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com)
2. Click the menu (☰) → **Settings** → **Connected apps**
3. Or, while using Gemini, ask about data in an app and Gemini will prompt you to connect
4. Click **Connect** for the app you want
5. Follow the steps to grant access

**How to disconnect:**
1. Go to Settings → Connected apps
2. Find the app to disconnect → click **Disconnect**

## Connect Google Workspace

**Google Workspace** connects Gmail, Drive, Docs, Sheets, and Calendar all at once.

**How to connect:**
1. Go to gemini.google.com
2. Menu (☰) → **Settings** → **Connected apps** → **Google Workspace**
3. Click **Connect** and grant access

**You must turn on Keep Activity** for Gemini to access data in Google Workspace.

---

## Personalization through Connected Apps

Once you've connected apps, Gemini can tailor answers to you based on:
- Data in Gmail — knows who you're talking with, what work
- Data in Calendar — knows your schedule
- Data in Drive — knows what documents you have

Reference: [About Personalization with Connected Apps](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16836988)

## Import a GitHub Repository

Besides ordinary Connected Apps, you can import code from GitHub for Gemini to analyze:

1. Click **Add files** → **More Uploads** → **Import Code**
2. Paste the GitHub repository URL
3. Gemini pulls the code to analyze right away

Reference: [Import GitHub Repository](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16176929)

## Using Connected Apps with a work/school account

For work or school account users:
- The administrator (admin) must enable it first
- Some apps may be unavailable depending on the organization's policy

## Control smart home

Connect **Google Home** with Gemini to control home devices by voice or text, e.g.:
```
"Turn off the bedroom lights"
"Set the AC to 25 degrees"
"Turn on the TV in the living room"
```

Reference: [Smart Home with Gemini](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/15335456)

## Manage appointments and the calendar

Once Google Calendar is connected:
```
"What appointments do I have tomorrow?"
"Add a team meeting Monday at 10"
"Cancel my Friday 2 PM appointment"
```

Reference: [Calendar with Gemini](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/15305236)

## Summary

Connected Apps make Gemini a "hub" connecting all your apps. Instead of switching between several apps, you can ask Gemini in one place and have it work in Gmail, Calendar, Drive, or other apps for you.
