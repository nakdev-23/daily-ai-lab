# 15 — Gemini on mobile

Reference: [Get Started with Mobile](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/14554984) | [Mobile App Features](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/14579631) | [Availability](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/14579026)

---

## What is the Gemini Mobile App

The Gemini mobile app gives a fuller experience than the web app in several ways, especially voice use (Gemini Live), camera use, and access from the lock screen.

---

## Download and set up

### Android
1. Download **Gemini** from the [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.bard)
2. Open the app and log in with your Google account
3. Gemini asks whether you want it as the Default Assistant

### iOS (iPhone & iPad)
1. Download **Gemini** from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/google-gemini/id6477489729)
2. Open the app and log in

---

## Supported countries

The Gemini mobile app is supported in many countries, including **Thailand, Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia, the Philippines, Japan, Korea**, and others.

Check the list of supported countries and languages: [Mobile Availability](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/14579026)

## Gemini as Default Assistant on Android

On Android you can set Gemini as the Default Assistant instead of Google Assistant:

1. Phone Settings → **Apps** → **Default apps** → **Digital assistant app**
2. Choose **Gemini**

Once set, long-press **Home** or say "Hey Google" to open Gemini instead of Google Assistant.

---

## Gemini on the lock screen (Android)

When the phone is locked, you can still call Gemini by swiping up from the bottom corner (if set as the Default Assistant).

**Features usable on the lock screen:**
- Asking simple questions
- Setting an alarm or timer
- Playing music

Reference: [Lock Screen](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/14576209)

---

## Gemini widget on the home screen

Add a Gemini widget on the Home Screen to reach Gemini faster.

**How to add a widget (Android):**
1. Long-press the Home Screen → **Widgets**
2. Search for **Gemini** and choose the widget you want
3. Drag and drop it on the Home Screen
4. Adjust the size and color as you like

Reference: [Add Widget](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16179553)

---

## Screen Actions — have Gemini see the screen and help

On Android you can open Gemini and have it read what's on the screen and help you:

**Usage examples:**
- Open a map, then ask Gemini "Which nearby restaurant do you recommend?"
- Open an email, then ask "Summarize this email"
- Open a photo, then ask "What's in this picture?"

**How to use it:**
1. Long-press Home to open Gemini (must be set as the Default Assistant)
2. Gemini reads the screen, then you can ask/answer

Reference: [Screen Actions](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/15850607)

---

## Gemini on a Smartwatch

Gemini supports Wear OS on some smartwatches:
- Ask questions by voice from your wrist
- See answers on the watch face

Reference: [Gemini on Smartwatch](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16401122)

---

## Gemini on earbuds

On **Pixel Buds** and supported earbuds:
- Say "Hey Google, let's talk" to start Gemini Live
- Hear the answer through the earbuds directly

Reference: [Gemini on Headphones](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/15456140)

---

## Multi-step tasks on Android apps

A new feature that lets Gemini do multi-step work in Android apps for you, e.g.:
- Order something in an app
- Fill out a form
- Manage settings in various apps

Reference: [Multi-step Tasks on Android](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16940971)

---

## Remove or manage the Gemini app on Android

- **Pause it temporarily:** Settings → Apps → Gemini → **Disable**
- **Delete the app:** Settings → Apps → Gemini → **Uninstall**
- **Switch the Default Assistant back:** Settings → Apps → Default apps → Digital assistant → choose Google

Reference: [Manage Gemini on Android](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16938321)

---

## The Gemini app on Mac

Gemini has a separate macOS app:
- Download it from the App Store on Mac
- It works like the web app but as a native app

Reference: [Gemini on Mac](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/17011627)

---

## Summary

Gemini on mobile gives a fuller experience than the web, especially on Android, which supports use from the lock screen, Gemini Live, Screen Actions, widgets, and connecting to smartwatches and earbuds.
