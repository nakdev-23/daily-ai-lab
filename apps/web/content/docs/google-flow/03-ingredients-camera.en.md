# Ingredients & Camera Controls 🎬

> Adapted from the official documentation at [labs.google/flow](https://labs.google/flow/about)

Two tools that make Flow better than randomly generating video — controlling continuity and camera direction.

## 🧪 Ingredients — reusable "ingredients"

**Ingredients** are elements (characters, objects, scenes, styles) you define and reuse across several shots to keep their **look consistent**.

- Create a character once and have it appear the same in every scene
- Lock the visual style so the whole clip is consistent
- Solve the "AI video where the character's face changes every shot" problem

## 🎥 Camera Controls — directing the camera

Control the shot like a real director:

| Movement | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Pan** | Sweep the camera left-right |
| **Tilt** | Angle the camera up-down |
| **Zoom / Dolly** | Move toward/away from the subject |
| **Orbit** | Rotate around the subject |

## 💡 Tips

- Define Ingredients first, then direct the camera, to control the work systematically
- Specify the camera movement clearly in the prompt, e.g. "slowly zoom in on the face"
- Reuse the same Ingredients across several shots for continuity
