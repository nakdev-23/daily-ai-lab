## Account management

### Sign up for an OpenAI account
Using ChatGPT requires an OpenAI account. Sign up at [chatgpt.com](https://chatgpt.com)

**How to sign up:**
1. Go to [chatgpt.com](https://chatgpt.com) → hit **Sign Up**
2. Choose a sign-up method:
   - Email + Password
   - Google Account
   - Microsoft Account
   - Apple ID (iOS)
3. Verify your email (if you signed up with email)
4. Enter your name and date of birth
5. Done — you can start using it

### Set the language
Reference: [How to change your language setting in ChatGPT](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8077070-how-to-change-your-language-setting-in-chatgpt)

ChatGPT detects the language from your browser settings. To set it to Thai:
1. Set the browser language to Thai
2. ChatGPT may show the UI (User Interface — the menus, buttons, text you interact with) in Thai in some parts, depending on the supported region

## Billing & Subscription

### How to manage your subscription
Reference: [How do I resolve a billing issue?](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6943700-how-do-i-resolve-a-billing-issue)

1. Go to **Settings → Manage Subscription** on the web
2. From here you can:
   - View your current plan details
   - Upgrade or change plans
   - Cancel your subscription
   - Download an invoice (a document listing the charges)

### Get an invoice
- **Web/Desktop**: Settings → Manage Subscription → download invoice
- **iOS App Subscriber**: you must view the invoice through the Apple App Store directly
- Reference: [How do I obtain my invoice if I subscribed from App Store?](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/7951521)

### Cancel your subscription
- **Web**: Settings → Manage Subscription → Cancel Plan
- **iOS**: cancel through the App Store → Your Apple ID → Subscriptions
- **Android**: cancel through the Google Play Store → Subscriptions
- After canceling, you can still use it until the end of the billing cycle

### Payment problems
Common issues:
- Card declined: check your card details, limit, or ask your bank
- Charged twice: contact Support with a screenshot attached
- Card not supported: use a card that supports international transactions

## Data Controls — control your data
Reference: [Data Controls FAQ](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/7730893-data-controls-faq)

### What this topic is
Data Controls (settings that let you define how ChatGPT handles your data) are settings that let you control how ChatGPT uses your conversation data.

### Main settings

**"Improve the model for everyone"**
- When on: OpenAI may use your conversations to train (the process by which AI learns from data to improve its abilities) the AI further
- When off: conversations are still saved in your history but not used for training

**How to turn it off (on Web):**
1. Click your profile → **Settings**
2. Go to **Data Controls**
3. Turn off **"Improve the model for everyone"**

**How to turn it off (on Mobile):**
1. Open the sidebar → tap your profile
2. Choose **Data Controls**
3. Turn off the toggle

### Worth knowing
- This setting syncs (updates simultaneously across all devices) between all your devices
- On the **Team, Enterprise, Edu** plans, data is not used to train the model by default
- Turning off Memory/Personalization does **not** turn off safety features (systems that prevent harmful responses) that need some context to respond safely

## Data privacy
Reference: [How Your Data is Used to Improve Model Performance](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/5722486-how-your-data-is-used-to-improve-model-performance)

### How OpenAI uses your data

**Data that may be used for training (if enabled):**
- Text conversations
- Uploaded files
- Transcripts (text converted from audio) of voice conversations
- Feedback (rating — thumbs up/down on whether you liked an answer)

**Data not used for training (by default):**
- Audio clips from voice chat
- Video clips from voice chat
- All data on the Team, Enterprise, Edu plans

**Rights you have:**
- Turn off using your data for training
- Export (download) your own data
- Delete your account and all data

### About the API
OpenAI **does not use API data** (data sent through the developer channel) for training (since March 1, 2023). API data is kept for 30 days, then deleted.

## Retention of chats and files
Reference: [How are Files vs Chats Retained?](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8983778-how-are-files-vs-chats-retained)

### Chats
- Chats are saved in your account until you delete them
- Delete a chat → it's removed from the system within **30 days**
- Delete your account → removed from the system within **30 days**
- **Exception**: chats that have been anonymized (de-identified — separated from your account but the content kept) may still be retained

### Files
- Files uploaded in a chat are kept as long as the chat exists
- Delete the chat → the file is deleted within 30 days
- Files in a Custom GPT → kept until you delete the GPT
- Files processed via Data Analysis: the duration depends on your plan

### Archive vs Delete
- **Archive**: the chat stays in the system but doesn't show in the main history — like putting it in a storage box, still there but out of sight
- **Delete**: a real deletion; data is removed within 30 days and cannot be recovered

### Audio/Video from voice chat
- Kept for 30 days along with the chat
- Delete the chat → the audio/video is deleted within 30 days

## Export your data
Reference: [How do I export my ChatGPT history and data?](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/7260999-how-do-i-export-my-chatgpt-history-and-data)

### How to export
1. Go to **Settings → Data Controls**
2. Click **Export Data**
3. Confirm via email
4. OpenAI sends a .zip file (a compressed file — combining several files into one smaller file) to your email (it may take a while)

### Data you get in the export
- Your full chat history (.html or .json files — data file formats that programs can read)
- Account data
- Note: **Shared Links are not included in the export**

## Account security

### Account hacked or accessed without permission
Reference: [I'm seeing unrecognized activity on my OpenAI account](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6986626)

**Do immediately:**
1. **Change your password** at [account.openai.com](https://account.openai.com)
2. **Sign out** on all devices (Settings → Sign Out All Devices)
3. Check your email for any unusual use
4. If your email was also accessed, change your email password too, quickly
5. Contact [OpenAI Support](https://help.openai.com)

### Prevent unauthorized access
- Use a strong password not reused elsewhere
- Turn on Two-Factor Authentication, or 2FA (you must enter an extra code from your phone after the password, for two layers of security), if available
- Beware of phishing emails (deceptive emails impersonating OpenAI to steal your data)

## Troubleshooting — common problems
Reference: [ChatGPT Error Messages](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6897133-chatgpt-error-messages)

### Common error messages and fixes

**"Something went wrong"**
- Problem: the server (the computer that provides the service) has a temporary issue
- Fix: refresh (reload the page) or wait a moment and try again
- Check: [status.openai.com](https://status.openai.com)

**"There was a problem preparing your chat"**
- Problem: the conversation can't load
- Fix: clear your browser's cache (temporary data the browser keeps to load pages faster)/cookies (small bits of data a site saves on your machine), try Incognito Mode (a fresh browser window with no old cache or cookies)

**"We detect suspicious activity"**
- Problem: your IP (network address number) or behavior is flagged
- Fix: change networks, turn off your VPN (a program that changes your IP) if you're using one, try a different network
- If it persists: contact Support

**"Our systems have detected unusual activity from your system"**
- Similar to the above, caused by rate limiting (limiting frequency — preventing too many requests in a short time) or the security system
- Fix: stop using it for a moment, then try again

**ChatGPT responds slowly**
Reference: [Why is my ChatGPT taking so long to respond?](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/7965556)
- During peak hours (when many users are on at once), the server may be slow
- Switch to a faster model, e.g. GPT-4o mini
- Check the system status: [status.openai.com](https://status.openai.com)

**CAPTCHA (a test to verify you're human) appears often**
Reference: [CAPTCHAs in ChatGPT](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8098338-captchas-in-chatgpt)
- Normal: used to verify you're human, not a bot (an automated program)
- If it appears very often: try disabling browser extensions, e.g. an ad blocker
- Try a different browser

**Network problems / network recommendations**
Reference: [Network recommendations for ChatGPT errors on web](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8498848)

ChatGPT needs:
- A stable internet connection
- No firewall (a network-protection system that filters connections)/proxy (a connection intermediary) blocking OpenAI's domains
- If using a VPN, some may cause problems; try disconnecting and reconnecting
- If a corporate network has issues, contact your IT admin to whitelist (specially allow — add to the not-blocked list) OpenAI's domains

## Safety — safety and Content Policy

### How far ChatGPT will answer
ChatGPT has limits on:
- **Harmful** content, e.g. how to make weapons, CSAM (child sexual abuse material — absolutely illegal)
- **Illegal** content
- Content that **harasses** or **threatens**
- **Impersonation**

### Does ChatGPT tell the truth
Reference: [Does ChatGPT tell the truth?](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8313428-does-chatgpt-tell-the-truth)

ChatGPT tries to give accurate information, but:
- It can **hallucinate** (produce information that sounds believable but isn't real, like a daydream — arising from the way the AI generates text)
- Its knowledge has an expiration, or knowledge cutoff (the AI doesn't know events after that date)
- Always verify important information from other sources
- Use Search Mode to get more accurate real-time (current) information

### Ads in ChatGPT
Reference: [Ads in ChatGPT](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001047-ads-in-chatgpt)

- Some ChatGPT platforms may have ads (advertisements)
- You have the right to control your ads experience in Settings → Data Controls
