# Pan — expand the image in various directions

> Main reference: [Pan](https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/32570788043405-Pan)

---

## What is Pan

Pan (expanding the image in one direction — left, right, up, or down, by adding new content in that direction) differs from Zoom Out in that it expands directionally, not all around.

---

## Pan directions

- **Pan Left** — expand to the left
- **Pan Right** — expand to the right
- **Pan Up** — expand upward
- **Pan Down** — expand downward

---

## How to use

### On the website
1. Upscale the image first
2. Choose the **arrow** for the direction you want (←↑→↓)
3. Wait for the result

### On Discord
After Upscaling:
- Press the arrow button that appears under the image

---

## Usage examples

### Create a Panoramic (a very wide image, usually a landscape)
```
Original image: a mountain landscape 1:1
Pan Left → Pan Right: get a very wide Panoramic image
```

### Add elements to the sides
```
Original image: a person standing in a garden
Pan Right: may reveal another part of the garden to the right
```

### Create a Vertical Story
```
Original image: a wide-angle city
Pan Up: expand up to see the sky or tall buildings
Pan Down: expand down to see the street or lower detail
```

---

## Pan + Prompt

In Pan Mode you can specify a Prompt for the new area:
1. Click Pan and look at the Prompt box
2. Add a description for the expanded area

---

## Pan repeatedly

You can Pan repeatedly to create a very wide image:
```
Pan Right → Pan Right → Pan Right
```
→ get a very long horizontal image, good for a Banner

---

## Things to know about Pan

- **Pan isn't symmetric** — the panned side may have different detail
- **Seams** — sometimes you may see a seam between the original part and the added part
- **Try several times** — each Pan gives a different result

---

## Comparing Pan with Zoom Out

| | Pan | Zoom Out |
|--|-----|---------|
| Expand direction | 1 direction | all directions |
| Original image | stays in place (just shifts) | stays in the center (smaller) |
| Good for | Panoramic, Banner | seeing a wider context |

---

## Real Use Case examples

### Create an advertising Banner
```
A 1:1 product image → Pan Right 2 times → get a wide horizontal image for a Banner
```

### Create Cover Art
```
A 1:1 Character image → Pan Down → see the lower body
```

### Landscape Panoramic
```
A 1:1 landscape image → Pan Left → Pan Right → get a 3:1 Panoramic image
```

---

## Summary

Pan is good for creating Panoramic, Widescreen, or expanding an image in a specific direction. It works best after Upscaling. You can Pan repeatedly to create an ever longer or taller image.
