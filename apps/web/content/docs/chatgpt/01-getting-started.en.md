## What is ChatGPT
Reference: [What is ChatGPT?](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6783457-what-is-chatgpt)

### What this topic is
ChatGPT is an AI assistant (artificial intelligence — a computer that learns and can respond like a human) developed by OpenAI. It uses a Large Language Model (an AI program trained on vast amounts of text so it can understand and produce language naturally) that has been trained to interact with humans naturally.

### What it's used for
ChatGPT helps with a wide range of tasks, such as answering questions, writing articles, summarizing documents, writing code (sets of program instructions), translating languages, analyzing data, and conversing like you're talking with an expert.

### Key details
ChatGPT understands natural language and can follow complex instructions, remembers context (the connections within a conversation — e.g. remembering what you asked earlier) within the same conversation, and adapts its responses to the situation.

Core abilities:
- **Answer questions** and explain concepts across many fields
- **Write and edit content**, e.g. emails, articles, reports
- **Offer creative suggestions**, e.g. writing short stories or brainstorming ideas
- **Solve problems with reasoning** (logical reasoning — thinking through steps logically)
- **Translate languages** across many of the world's languages, including Thai

### Cautions
ChatGPT can sometimes give incorrect information; you should verify facts from other sources too, especially information that requires high accuracy, such as medical or legal information.

### Summary
ChatGPT is an AI chatbot (an intelligent conversation program — it responds via typing or speaking) that can help with anything, suitable for both beginners and professionals.

### What this topic is
This page explains all the features (the abilities or properties of a program) ChatGPT offers, organized by type of function.

### Core capabilities

ChatGPT has these foundational abilities:

- **Answer questions and explain things**, whether science, history, math, or everyday matters
- **Write, edit, or summarize content** in many formats
- **Give creative suggestions**, e.g. writing fiction or helping brainstorm ideas

- **Translate** between different languages

Paid-plan users can choose among different models (Model — a version of the AI with different abilities, like phone models) as needed.

## How to access ChatGPT

### Via the website
Available at [chatgpt.com](https://chatgpt.com) or [chat.openai.com](https://chat.openai.com)
- Free with an OpenAI account
- Works on any browser (web-browsing program — e.g. Chrome, Safari, Firefox) updated to its latest version

### Mobile apps
- **iOS**: Download from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/) (the app is called "ChatGPT")
- **Android**: Download from the [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/)

### Desktop apps
- **macOS**: Requires macOS 14+ and Apple Silicon (a processor chip Apple designed itself — M1 or better). Download at [openai.com/chatgpt/desktop](https://openai.com/chatgpt/desktop/). Press **⌥ (Option) + Space** to open chat anytime.
- **Windows**: There's a Windows app too

### Supported countries
Reference: [ChatGPT Supported Countries](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6723741-chatgpt-supported-countries-and-territories)

ChatGPT is available in most countries and territories worldwide, including **Thailand**. Check the full list at the reference link.

## Subscription plans (Plans & Pricing)

### ChatGPT Free
Reference: [Using ChatGPT's Free Tier - FAQ](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9275200-using-chatgpts-free-tier-faq)

#### What this is
The free plan lets beginners try ChatGPT at no cost.

#### Details
- Access to the **GPT-5.5 mini** model mainly (a lighter version of GPT-5.5 — faster but less capable)
- Can use GPT-5.5 but with usage limits
- Supports Voice Mode with a limit of up to 2 hours per day
- Limited file uploads (up to 3 files per day)
- Partial use of GPTs
- **Does not support** Memory (cross-conversation memory), Deep Research, and some Canvas features

#### Worth knowing
The free plan suits those who want general trial use, but for full features you should upgrade (raise your plan tier) to Plus or Pro.

---

### ChatGPT Plus
Reference: [What is ChatGPT Plus?](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/7232945-what-is-chatgpt-plus)

#### What this is
ChatGPT Plus is a monthly subscription priced at **$20/month** for general users who want more features.

#### What you get
- Full access to **GPT-5.5** (much higher limits than the free plan)
- Access to **o1, o3, o4-mini** (models designed to "think" before answering — good for complex work)
- Use of **Memory** (ChatGPT remembers your info across conversations, so you don't repeat yourself)
- **Voice Mode** with GPT-5.5, nearly unlimited
- Upload up to **20 files per Project**
- Use **DALL·E** (an AI model that creates images from descriptions) to generate images
- Full **Canvas**
- **Deep Research** a limited number of times per month
- **Custom GPTs**
- Access to the **GPT Store** (a library of GPTs from developers worldwide)

#### How to subscribe
1. Go to [chatgpt.com](https://chatgpt.com)
2. Click your profile → **Upgrade to Plus**
3. Pay by credit/debit card

#### Cancel
Cancel anytime in Settings → Manage Subscription. Those who subscribed via the iOS App must cancel through Apple's App Store instead.

### ChatGPT Pro
Reference: [What is ChatGPT Pro?](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9793128-what-is-chatgpt-pro)

#### What this is
ChatGPT Pro is the top-tier plan for heavy users, priced at **$200/month**.

#### What you get
- Everything in Plus **plus**:
- Use of **o1 Pro Mode**, which thinks more deeply and accurately than regular o1 (a special mode that takes longer to think for more accurate results)
- **Unlimited** GPT-5.5 Voice Mode (no fallback — switching to a backup model when quota runs out — to mini)
- More Deep Research than Plus
- The highest limits for every model and feature
- Suitable for researchers, developers, or anyone who uses AI intensively

#### Worth knowing
The Pro plan suits people who use ChatGPT as their main everyday work tool and want maximum performance.

### ChatGPT Team
Reference: [ChatGPT Team Plan FAQ](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8798636-chatgpt-team-plan-faq)

#### What this is
ChatGPT Team is a plan for teams or small organizations, priced at **$25–30/person/month** (depending on the number of members).

#### What you get
- Everything in Plus
- A shared Workspace (a shared work space — like an online office the team shares)
- **Team data is not used to train the model** (protecting company data)
- Manage members and permissions (rights that define who can do what)
- Higher limits than Plus
- An Admin Console (a control panel for administrators — managing members and various settings)

#### Who it's for
Teams of 2+ who want to collaborate and need data privacy.

---

### ChatGPT Enterprise
Reference: [What is ChatGPT Enterprise?](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8265053-what-is-chatgpt-enterprise)

#### What this is
ChatGPT Enterprise is the plan for large organizations needing maximum security and customization (pricing by direct inquiry with OpenAI).

#### What you get
- Everything in Team **plus**:
- Enterprise-grade Security and Privacy, certified to SOC 2 Type 2 (an international information-security standard for organizations)
- **Data is absolutely not used to train the model**
- A larger Context Window (the maximum amount of conversation the AI can "remember" in one session), supporting very complex work
- An advanced Admin Dashboard (a screen showing an overview and system controls)
- SSO, or Single Sign-On (one login that works across many services — convenient for organizations with many systems), supported
- A customizable API (Application Programming Interface — a channel that lets other programs connect to and use ChatGPT)
- **Visual Retrieval** (pulling data from images — reading data from charts or images inside a PDF) for PDFs
- Supports a **Flexible Pricing Plan** (pay according to actual usage)

## How to log in / login problems
Reference: [Why can't I log in to ChatGPT?](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6951536-why-can-t-i-log-in-to-chatgpt)

### How to log in
ChatGPT supports signing in via:
- **Email and password** (OpenAI Account)
- **Google Account**
- **Microsoft Account**
- **Apple ID** (on iOS)

### Common problems and fixes

**1. Can't log in at all**
- Check that your email and password are correct
- Try Reset Password (request a new password via email)
- Check whether a VPN (a program that changes your network location — making it look like you're using the internet from another country) or Proxy (an intermediary that relays internet data) is blocking the connection

**2. The message "We detect suspicious activity" appears**
- OpenAI detected unusual behavior from your IP (your device's address on the network — like a house number on the internet)
- Try a different Wi-Fi network, or turn off the VPN
- If it doesn't improve, contact [help.openai.com](https://help.openai.com)

**3. CAPTCHA (a test to verify you're human — e.g. picking images or typing distorted letters) appears often**
- ChatGPT uses CAPTCHA to prevent bots (automated, non-human programs)
- Complete the CAPTCHA as usual; if it appears very frequently, it may be caused by a browser extension (an add-on to your web browser) interfering — try disabling the extension and retry

**4. Account locked or unusual**
Reference: [I'm seeing unrecognized activity](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6986626)
- If you find unrecognized activity in your account, change your password immediately
- Sign out from all devices
- Contact OpenAI Support

### Worth knowing
Thailand is officially supported, but some areas or certain ISPs (internet service providers) may have connection issues; try a VPN if necessary (but a VPN can also get you flagged as suspicious activity).
