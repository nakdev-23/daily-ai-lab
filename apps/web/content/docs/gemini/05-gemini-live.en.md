# 05 — Gemini Live — talk naturally

Reference: [Talk naturally with Gemini Live](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/15274899)

---

## What is Gemini Live

Gemini Live lets you talk to Gemini by voice naturally, like talking with a real person. You can interrupt mid-answer, change topics instantly, or add more details anytime.

**What you can do with Gemini Live:**
- **Converse** — speak without typing, and Gemini replies by voice
- **Brainstorm out loud** — plan work, gift ideas, business
- **Explore topics** — learn and dig deep into things you're interested in
- **Practice speaking** — rehearse a presentation, practice a language, prepare for an interview

---

## What you need

- An **Android** or **iOS** phone or tablet
- The **Gemini** app on mobile
- A personal Google account, or a work/school account with access enabled
- Age **18+**
- The Google app updated to the latest version

> **Note:** Gemini Live isn't yet supported on the web app or Gemini in Messages.

---

## How to start using Gemini Live

1. Open the Gemini app on mobile
2. Tap the **Live** button (the microphone icon) at the bottom, or swipe left
3. The first time, follow the on-screen instructions
4. Just start speaking

**Other shortcuts:**
- Say "Hey Google, let's talk Live" or "Hey Google, let's talk"
- On Pixel Buds, say "Hey Google, let's talk"

---

## Controlling the Live conversation

| Action | How |
|---|---|
| Pause (Hold) | Tap the **Hold** button — the mic turns off |
| Resume | Tap the **Live** button again |
| Mute the mic | Tap **Mute** — Gemini can keep talking but won't hear you |
| End the conversation | Tap **End** or swipe right — you can view the transcript |
| Switch from Live to typing | Tap **End** in the Live chat → return to text mode |
| Switch from typing to Live | Tap the **Live** button in the regular chat |

## Sharing camera and screen in Gemini Live

### Share the camera
You can turn on the camera so Gemini sees what you see and talks about it.

- Tap **Camera** (the camera icon) to turn on the camera
- Tap **Switch** to toggle the front/rear camera
- The camera turns off automatically when you: tap Hold, leave the app, or lock the screen

### Share the screen
Let Gemini see what's on your screen and help you think together.

**How to share the screen:**
1. On the Live page, tap **Turn on screen sharing** (the screen icon) and follow the instructions
2. Or open Gemini by saying "Hey Google" then tap **Share screen with Live**

**How to stop sharing:**
- Return to the Gemini app and tap **Turn off screen sharing**
- Or swipe down from the top edge, tap the "Screen Sharing" card → **Stop sharing**

## Using Connected Apps in Gemini Live

Gemini Live can connect to various apps, e.g. adding an appointment in Calendar or finding nearby restaurants.

**Supported apps (partial):**

| Category | Supported apps |
|---|---|
| Google | Google Calendar, Google Tasks, Google Keep, Google Workspace |
| Entertainment | YouTube, YouTube Music, Spotify |
| Smart Home | Google Home |
| Travel/shopping | Google Hotels, Google Flights, Shopping |
| Phone brands | Samsung, OPPO, Vivo, Xiaomi, OnePlus, HONOR, TECNO |

> **Note:** Work/school accounts can use only Google Calendar, Google Keep, and Google Tasks.

## Voice and caption settings

### Change Gemini's voice
1. Open the Gemini app
2. Menu (☰) → profile picture → **Settings** → **Gemini's Voice**
3. Tap to listen to each voice → **Confirm**

### Turn on captions
1. Start a Live chat
2. Tap **Turn captions on** at the top right

### Adjust caption size and style
1. Menu (☰) → **Settings** → **Caption preferences** → **Caption size and style**

### Turn interrupting Gemini on/off
Gemini lets you speak over it while it's talking. To turn this off:
1. Menu (☰) → **Settings** → turn off **Interrupt Live responses**

---

## Using Gemini Live in the background

You can keep using your phone while Live is running:
- **Send Live to the background:** swipe up from the bottom edge
- **Return to Live:** swipe down from the top edge → tap the "Live with Gemini" card

**You must turn on notifications** to use Live in the background or while the screen is locked:
1. Phone Settings → **Notifications** → **App notifications** → **Google**
2. Turn on **All Google Notifications** and **Live with Gemini**

## Example phrases to use with Gemini Live

```
"I'm about to interview my first guest on a podcast — any tips?"

"My friend is really hard to buy gifts for — can you help me think of ideas?"

"What should I do to start the day well and feel productive? I have an important meeting today"
```

## Summary

Gemini Live changes how you communicate with AI from typing to natural conversation, supporting screen and camera sharing, connecting to many apps, and running in the background. It's good for practicing speaking, brainstorming, or work where you use your hands while talking.
