# Commercial use

> Main reference: [Using Images Videos Commercially](https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/27870375276557-Using-Images-Videos-Commercially)

---

## A summary of usage rights

### Paid-plan users (Basic, Standard, Pro, Mega)
- **Can use commercially** — sell, use in ads, make into products
- Receive a broad usage License
- Income from selling work doesn't need to be shared with Midjourney

---

## What you can do

- **Sell Prints** — print to sell, make into Posters, Canvas
- **Use in ads** — print media, online, Social Media
- **Use in products** — shirts, mugs, bags (Print-on-Demand)
- **Use in Client work** — create images for clients for a fee
- **NFT** — create Digital Art to sell as an NFT

---

## Limitations to know

### Cases that may have problems
- Images using a Real Person may have rights issues
- Images imitating the features of copyrighted IP work
- Images naming a specific artist or brand

### Recommendations
- Avoid Prompts that name a copyrighted brand or character
- If you must use it in a high-risk Context, consult a lawyer

---

## High-Revenue Companies

For companies with revenue over $1 million per year, there are special conditions:
- They must buy the Pro or Mega plan only
- See more details in the Terms of Service

---

## Attribution

Currently Midjourney **doesn't require** stating that something was created with Midjourney, but stating it is a Best Practice that many creators do.

---

## The License received

When you subscribe to a paid plan, you receive the rights:
- **A Non-exclusive License** — Midjourney and other users may create similar images
- **Usable worldwide** — no country restriction
- **Permanent** — the License has no expiration

---

## Case study: what you can and can't do

### You can
- Design a Book Cover and sell the eBook
- Use an image as a Background in a monetized YouTube Video
- Create images for a client's Website and charge a service fee
- Sell printed-design products online (Print-on-Demand)
- Use in Marketing Material for a business

### Be careful
- Images of people clearly resembling famous figures
- Images of clearly copyrighted characters, such as Marvel, Disney
- Images using a Prompt that names a specific brand or logo

---

## Advice for Freelancers

If you're a Freelancer (an independent worker — someone who works for several clients without a permanent employer) and take image-creation jobs for clients:

1. Use Midjourney Pro/Mega for Client work
2. Enable Stealth Mode for privacy
3. State in the contract with the client that AI was used to help create the images
4. Hand over the correct License to the client

---

## Summary

Paid-plan users have broad commercial-use rights — can sell, use in ads, do Print-on-Demand — but beware of real-person images and others' Intellectual Property (work the law protects). Read the Terms of Service carefully for high-risk cases.
