# Suno models — v4, v4.5, v5, and v5.5

Suno continuously develops its AI models for creating music. Each version has improved capabilities and new features added. This section explains the history and differences of each model.

---

## Suno Model Timeline

```
2024:
├── v3     — March 2024 (the first famous version)
├── v4     — November 2024 (a big leap)
└── v4.5   — May 2025 (Artist Update)

2025-2026:
├── v5     — September 2025 (the most significant leap)
└── v5.5   — March 2026 (Voices + Custom Models + My Taste)
```

---

## Suno v4 — What's New?

**v4** (November 2024) launched with:

- **Max song length 4 minutes** (up from v3)
- **Much better audio quality** — sounds more natural
- Improved **Vocal Clarity**
- More complex **Instrumental Detail**

---

## Suno v4.5 — Artist Update

**v4.5** (May 2025) focuses on giving users more control:

### New features in v4.5

| Feature | Description |
|--------|---------|
| **Creative Sliders** | adjust the Randomness and Creativity levels of the AI |
| **Better Prompts in Lyrics** | enter more detailed commands in the lyrics |
| **Audio Uploads** | bring your own audio in to use |
| **Song Editor** | edit various parts of a song more easily |
| **Stem Extraction** | separate the vocals from the music |
| **Add Vocals** | add vocals into an Instrumental song |
| **Inspire** | a button to generate new Prompt inspiration |
| **Detailed Style Instructions** | specify the Style in more detail than before |

### Creative Sliders

**Creative Sliders** have two axes:

```
Creativity:
Conservative ◄──────────────────► Experimental

Intensity:
Subtle ◄──────────────────► Intense
```

---

## Suno v5 — a significant leap

**v5** (September 2025) is what Suno calls "Intelligent Composition," first launched for Pro and Premier.

### New capabilities in v5

- **Intelligent Composition** — the AI understands musical structure at a deeper level
- **Songs up to 8 minutes** directly (no need to Extend multiple times)
- **Better Vocal Emotion** — the AI sings with feeling more seamlessly
- **Multi-genre Blending** — songs mixing multiple Genres sound natural
- **Improved Lyrics Alignment** — lyrics match the music better than before

### Remaster in v5

**Remaster** (creating an improved version from an existing song) in v5 lets you:

- Take an old song (v3 or v4) and Remaster it with the new model
- Get better quality sound without recreating it entirely
- Create a "Variation" (a slightly different version) of the existing song

---

## Suno v5.5 — Voices, Custom Models, My Taste

**v5.5** (March 2026) adds more Personalization:

### Voices — use your voice

**Voices** lets you:

- Record your own voice and use it in Suno
- Create songs where the AI sings with a voice Inspired by yours
- Adjust the Vocal Tone and Character as you wish

> **Important:** Suno has measures to prevent using others' voices without permission.

### Custom Models

**Custom Models** in v5.5 let you:

- "Train" (teach the AI to learn your music style) your own model
- Save your Sonic Identity
- Consistently create songs with "my sound"

### My Taste

**My Taste** is a system that:

- Learns from songs you Like or create often
- Adjusts Recommendations to match your preferences
- Helps the Create Page suggest Styles you're likely to enjoy

## How to choose a model

On the Create Page you can choose a Model (for Pro+ plans):

1. Click the **Model** or **Version** button on the Create page
2. Choose the model you want
3. The latest model (v5.5) is the Default

### When should you use an older model?

- **v4** — when you want the "Classic Suno" sound that old users are familiar with
- **v4.5** — when you want Audio Upload but not v5's Complexity
- **v5** — for high-quality work and longer songs
- **v5.5** — when you want maximum Personalization

## Model Availability Update

Per the Suno Model Availability Update (September 2025):

- **v3** was removed from Create and is still accessible through old songs
- **v4 and v4.5** are still usable
- **v5** opened to Pro/Premier first, then to everyone
- **v5.5** launched in March 2026
