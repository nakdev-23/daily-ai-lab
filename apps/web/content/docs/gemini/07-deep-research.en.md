# 07 — Deep Research — automated in-depth research

Reference: [Use Deep Research](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/15719111)

---

## What is Deep Research

**Deep Research** is a feature that lets Gemini search and analyze data from many sources at once, then summarize it into a complete report. By default it uses **Google Search** as the data source, but you can add other sources, e.g. Gmail or Google Drive.

It differs from a regular question in that Deep Research actually "goes out and finds data" for you, rather than answering from the model's knowledge alone.

---

## What you need

- Age **18+**
- Logged in to Gemini Apps

---

## The model Deep Research uses

| Plan | Model used |
|---|---|
| **Free, Plus** | Thinking |
| **Pro, Ultra** | Pro (higher quality) |

---

## How to create a Deep Research report

1. Go to [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com)
2. In the message box, click **Tools** → **Deep Research** (the magnifying-glass icon)
3. (Optional) click **Add files** to attach files or images
4. (Optional) click **Sources** to choose data sources:
   - **Google Search** — included by default
   - **Gmail / Drive** — you must connect Google Workspace first
   - Turn off Google Search to limit the search to only the sources you chose
5. Type the question or topic you want to research
6. Click **Submit** → Gemini creates a **Research Plan** for you to review first
7. (Optional) click **Edit plan** to adjust the research plan
8. Click **Start research**
9. When the report is done, click **Open** to view it

**Time taken:** about 5–10 minutes per report (may be longer for complex topics)

**While you wait:** you can leave the chat page; Gemini notifies you when the report is done.

---

## Deep Research limits

| Plan | Number of reports |
|---|---|
| **Free** | 5 reports/month |
| **Google AI Plus** | 12 reports/day |
| **Google AI Pro** | 20 reports/day |
| **Google AI Ultra** | 120 reports/day |

There's also a limit on how many research reports you can run at the same time.

---

## Visuals & Animations in reports (Ultra only)

**Google AI Ultra** users get reports with images and animations, e.g.:
- Charts
- Diagrams
- Interactive simulators

> **Note:** This feature isn't supported when you choose Gmail/Drive as the data source.

---

## Find and manage Deep Research reports

1. Go to gemini.google.com
2. Click the menu (☰) to see your chat list
3. Find the chat with the research report in the **Recent** section

> **You must turn on Keep Activity** to access old reports.

---

## Listen to a report with Audio Overview

Instead of reading, you can have Gemini read the report aloud podcast-style:

1. Open the chat with the Deep Research report
2. In the Canvas panel on the right, click **Create** → **Audio Overview**

---

## Show the report as a Visualization

1. Open the chat with the report
2. In the Canvas panel, click **Create**
3. Type a prompt describing the kind of visualization you want

---

## Share and export a report

1. Open the chat with the report
2. In the Canvas panel, click **Share & export**
3. Choose how you want to:
   - **Share Canvas** — create a public link
   - **Export to Docs** — export to Google Docs
   - **Copy Contents** — copy the text

---

## Examples of topics suited to Deep Research

```
"Analyze the EV market trends in Southeast Asia for 2025–2026"

"Compare frameworks for building web apps: React, Vue, Angular"

"The history and development of AI from 1950 to the present"

"The impact of climate change on agriculture in Thailand"
```

---

## Summary

Deep Research suits work needing data from many sources, in-depth analysis, or a comprehensive report. Instead of spending hours searching yourself, Deep Research gives you a complete report within 5–10 minutes.
