# 17 — Troubleshooting and reporting problems

Reference: [Troubleshooting](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13275746) | [Delete App Data](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/15842699) | [Send Feedback](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13278997)

## Common problems and how to fix them

### 1. Gemini doesn't respond / the page hangs

**Possible causes:**
- An internet connection problem
- The browser cache is full
- Google's servers have a temporary issue

**How to fix:**
1. Reload the page (Ctrl+R / Cmd+R)
2. Clear the browser cache
3. Try a different browser
4. Check the service status at the [Google Status Dashboard](https://www.google.com/appsstatus)

---

### 2. The error message "Something went wrong"

**How to fix:**
1. Refresh the page
2. Try signing out and signing in again
3. Clear cookies and cache
4. If it still doesn't work, wait a moment and try again

---

### 3. Can't upload files

**Messages you might see:**
- "Delete data to upload file" — old data is stored; delete old files in the conversation first
- "You've reached your limit" — over the allowed number of files (10 files/prompt)
- "Your uploads may be too large" — the file exceeds the limit (generally 100MB, video 2GB)

**How to fix:**
- Reduce the file size before uploading
- Upload one file at a time instead of several at once
- Start a new chat

---

### 4. Gemini Live doesn't work

**Check:**
- The account must be over 18
- Used on the mobile app only (not supported on the web)
- The microphone must be allowed in the app

**How to fix:**
1. Check microphone permission: phone Settings → Privacy → Microphone → Gemini
2. Close and reopen the app
3. Update the Gemini app to the latest version

---

### 5. Some features don't appear (e.g. Deep Research, Canvas, Gems)

**Possible causes:**
- Work/school accounts have restrictions
- The feature requires a higher plan
- The feature isn't supported in some countries
- The account is under the required age

**How to fix:**
- Check your current plan
- See the [features-by-plan table](03-โมเดลและแผนบริการ.md)
- Try using a personal account instead of a work account

---

### 6. Gemini answers incorrectly / wrong information

**What you should know:**
- Gemini may "hallucinate" or give incorrect information
- Always verify important information from other sources

**How to handle it:**
- Click **Thumbs down** (👎) on the incorrect answer
- Type "This answer doesn't seem right, can you check again?"
- Try rephrasing the question more clearly

---

### 7. Chats disappear or no chat history found

**Cause:**
- Keep Activity is off (chats aren't saved)
- You used Temporary Chat
- The history was already deleted

**How to fix:**
- Check Activity at myactivity.google.com/product/gemini
- Turn on Keep Activity in Settings so it doesn't happen again

---

### 8. Login problems

**How to fix:**
1. Try signing out and signing in again
2. Check you're using the correct Google account
3. If you can't log in, check at [accounts.google.com](https://accounts.google.com)

---

## How to send feedback and report problems

### Send feedback in the app

**How to send feedback:**
1. On the problematic answer, click **Thumbs down** (👎)
2. Choose the problem category
3. Add details (if you want)
4. Click **Submit**

Or:
- Web app: menu (☰) → **Settings & help** → **Send feedback**
- Mobile: menu (☰) → **Help & feedback** → **Send feedback**

Reference: [Send Feedback](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13278997)

---

### Report inappropriate content

If Gemini creates inappropriate or harmful content:
1. Click **Thumbs down** → choose "Offensive or unsafe"
2. Or report via: [Report a Gemini Concern](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16719589)

---

### Report a violation

If you find a violation of Gemini's policies:
- [Report here](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16719589)

---

## How to delete Gemini app data

### Delete chat history
1. Go to gemini.google.com → Settings → Activity
2. Click **Delete** on the chat you want
3. Or delete all at once

### Delete Gemini Agent data (Remote Browser Data)
If you use the Gemini Agent and want to clear data:
1. Settings → **Gemini Agent** → **Delete remote browser data**

### Delete the app on Android
- Settings → Apps → Gemini → **Uninstall**
- Or **Disable** (if it's a built-in app)

Reference: [Delete App Data](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/15842699)

## Check Google service status

If you suspect the problem is Google's servers, not your machine:
- Check the status at the [Google Workspace Status Dashboard](https://www.google.com/appsstatus)

## Summary

Most problems with Gemini can be fixed by reloading the page, clearing the cache, or checking your plan. For more complex problems, you can send feedback via the Thumbs down button or contact the Google Help Center directly.
