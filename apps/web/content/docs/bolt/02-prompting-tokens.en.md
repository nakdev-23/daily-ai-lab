# Prompting & Tokens ⚡

> Adapted from the official documentation at [support.bolt.new](https://support.bolt.new/)

## ✍️ Prompting Bolt effectively

- **Start with the big structure**, then add features one at a time
- **Be specific** — describe the UI, the data, and the behavior you want
- **One chunk at a time** — edit/add one thing, check it works before moving on
- If you hit an error, tell Bolt what the error is and it will help fix it

Example:
> "Build a to-do list site with React, with add/delete/check-done, saving to localStorage"

## 🪙 What are tokens

**Tokens** are Bolt's unit of AI usage — every time you have the AI write/edit code, it uses tokens by the amount of work (each plan has a monthly quota).

## 💰 Token-saving tips

- **Give one clear instruction** rather than a vague one you fix many times over
- **Make targeted edits** when tweaking small details, instead of rebuilding the whole page
- Combine several small requests into one clear instruction
- Check the result before instructing further, so you don't have to backtrack
