# Video Translation — one clip, many languages 🌍

> Compiled in English from the official docs at [docs.heygen.com](https://docs.heygen.com/), the Video Translation section

**Video Translation** turns a video of someone talking into one that **speaks another language** by re-dubbing the audio and adjusting the lip movement (lip-sync) to match the new language — letting your content reach people worldwide from a single clip.

## ⚙️ How it works (overview)

1. Transcribe the original audio to text
2. Translate it into the target language
3. Re-dub the audio (keeping the tone close)
4. Adjust the **lip-sync** so the lips match the new audio

## 📖 Configurable values

| Value | Description |
|---|---|
| **Target language** | The language to translate into (dozens to choose from) |
| **Number of speakers** | How many speakers are in the clip |
| **Caption** | Whether to add captions (subtitles) too |

## 💡 Tips

- Use a clip with a clearly visible face and crisp audio for a better lip-sync result
- Clips with a single speaker are usually more accurate than several speakers
- Check the translation before publishing (some phrasing may need adjusting)

## ▶️ Steps

1. Go to the **Video Translation** menu and upload a video (or paste a link)
2. Choose the target language and number of speakers
3. Click generate, then wait for processing
4. Check the result, then download
