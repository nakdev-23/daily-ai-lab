# 06 — Gems — custom AI assistants

Reference: [Get Started with Gems](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/15236321) | [Use Gems](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/15146780) | [Share Gems](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16504957)

---

## What are Gems

**Gems** are specially configured versions of Gemini, where you can create a Gem with the personality, expertise, and answering approach you want.

Picture a Gem as a personal assistant that "knows" your work well enough, e.g. a Gem for writing emails, a Gem for teaching Japanese, or a Gem for analyzing marketing data.

---

## What you need

- Age 13+ (or the age set in your country) for a personal account
- You must be logged in to Gemini Apps
- Some features aren't supported on the mobile app yet

---

## Premade Gems — ready-made Gems from Google

Google has ready-made Gems for immediate use, covering many areas, e.g.:

| Gem | Role |
|---|---|
| **Brainstormer** | Brainstorm and explore new ideas |
| **Career guide** | Give career-path advice |
| **Coding partner** | Help write and debug code |
| **Learning coach** | Teach concepts and explain content |
| **Writing editor** | Edit writing, adjust tone |

**How to use a Premade Gem:**
1. Go to [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com)
2. Click the menu (☰) → **Gems**
3. Choose a Gem from the Premade list

## Create your own Custom Gems

Besides Premade Gems, you can create your own specialized Gem.

### Steps to create a Custom Gem

1. Go to [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com)
2. Click the menu (☰) → **Gems** → **+ New Gem** (or Create a Gem)
3. Name it and write **Instructions** (commands for the Gem)
4. Click **Save**

### How to write good Instructions

Instructions are the heart of a Gem; the clearer the better. You should specify:

- **Role:** who is this Gem? (e.g. "you're an English coach")
- **What to do:** the main task each time
- **What not to do:** what to avoid
- **Answer format:** short/long, formal/informal, the language to use

**Example Instructions:**
```
You are a proofreader who is an English expert.
Your job:
- Check grammar and spelling
- Suggest more suitable words
- Explain why you made each change
- Keep the writer's tone and style
Always answer in Thai, even though the content is in English
```

### Add files to a Gem

You can attach documents, a style guide, or reference data to a Gem to give it fuller context.

---

## Using Gems

1. Go to gemini.google.com
2. Click the menu (☰) → **Gems** → choose the Gem you want
3. Type your question or task

**Tips:**
- Each Gem carries its context from the Instructions, so you don't have to retype the command every time
- You can have several Gems for different jobs
- Gems aren't supported in Temporary Chat

---

## Share a Gem with others

You can share a Gem you created with others:

1. Go to the Gem you want to share
2. Click the **Share** button
3. Copy the link and send it to others

Whoever gets the link sees the Gem and its Instructions (read-only, not editable).

## Gems from Google Labs — Mini-apps & Custom Workflows

Google Labs has a special option to create a Gem as a **Mini-app** or **Custom Workflow**, which is more complex than a regular Gem and supports automated multi-step work.

Reference: [Create AI mini-apps](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16802014)

## Examples of useful Gem ideas

| Gem name | Brief Instructions |
|---|---|
| **Email Drafter** | Write professional emails from the content given |
| **Thai English Tutor** | Teach English in Thai, with examples |
| **Code Reviewer** | Review code for bugs and suggest best practices |
| **Meeting Summarizer** | Summarize key points and action items from a meeting |
| **Social Media Writer** | Write captions for Instagram/Facebook |

---

## Cautions

- Gems can't be used with **Gemini Live** at the moment
- If you delete a Gem with attached files, you may have to wait a moment for the storage to come back
- Gems store data in Gemini Apps Activity; if you turn off Keep Activity, they can't be saved

---

## Summary

Gems turn Gemini into a specialized assistant that knows your work, whether writing, code, teaching, or data analysis. Building a good Custom Gem requires writing clear Instructions and attaching complete reference data.
