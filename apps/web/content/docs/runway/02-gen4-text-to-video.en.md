# Gen-4 / Gen-4.5 — create video from text (Text-to-Video)

> **Gen-4.5** is Runway's latest AI video-generation model, hailed as "the best video model in the world" at present.

---

## What is Gen-4.5?

**Gen-4.5** (Generation 4.5 — Runway's 4.5th-generation video model) is an AI model that can create high-resolution videos from text alone, or from an image + accompanying text.

### Gen-4.5's main capabilities:
- Create video from a pure text description (Text-to-Video)
- Turn images into videos (Image-to-Video)
- Supports videos 5-10 seconds long
- Maximum resolution 1280x720 (Landscape) or 720x1280 (Portrait)

### Models in the Gen-4 family:
| Model | Speed | Quality | Credits/second |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Gen-4.5** | Medium | Highest | 12 credits |
| **Gen-4 Turbo** | Fast | Good | 5 credits |

---

## How to use Text-to-Video

### Step 1: Choose the tool

1. Log in to Runway
2. On the Home page, click **"Video"** or **"Text/Image to Video"**
3. Choose the **Gen-4.5** model from the top menu

### Step 2: Write a Prompt

The **Prompt** (a description or instruction for the AI) is the heart of creating a video.

**A good Prompt structure:**
```
[Subject] + [Action] + [Setting] + [Style/Mood] + [Camera movement]
```

**Examples of good Prompts:**

```
A golden retriever puppy running through a field of sunflowers, 
warm afternoon light, cinematic, slow motion, tracking shot
```

```
A futuristic city at night with neon lights reflecting on wet streets, 
cyberpunk aesthetic, aerial drone shot, rain
```

### Step 3: Adjust the settings

**Duration** (the video length):
- **5 seconds** — uses fewer Credits, good for testing
- **10 seconds** — a longer video, uses more Credits

**Aspect Ratio** (the width-to-height proportion):
- **16:9** (1280x720) — standard Landscape, good for YouTube, TV
- **9:16** (720x1280) — Portrait, good for TikTok, Instagram Reels

### Step 4: Generate

1. Click the **"Generate"** button
2. Wait for the AI to process (about 30-90 seconds)
3. View the result

## Techniques for writing effective Prompts

### Words that make videos look good

**Camera Movement:**
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `dolly in` | The camera moves toward the subject |
| `pan left/right` | The camera turns left/right |
| `tracking shot` | The camera follows a moving subject |
| `aerial shot` | A high-angle shot |
| `close-up` | A close-range image |
| `wide shot` | A wide-angle image |

**Visual Style:**
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `cinematic` | A film style |
| `photorealistic` | Realistic like a real photo |
| `anime style` | Japanese cartoon style |
| `oil painting` | An oil-painting style |
| `8K ultra HD` | Very high resolution |

**Lighting & Atmosphere:**
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `golden hour` | Light around sunset |
| `soft lighting` | Soft light |
| `dramatic lighting` | Dramatic light |
| `foggy` | Foggy |
| `volumetric light` | Light with volume, looking deep |

## Example Prompts and results

### Example 1: Nature
**A good Prompt:**
```
Majestic waterfall in a lush tropical forest, 
mist rising, golden hour lighting, 
slow motion water flow, cinematic wide shot
```

**A Prompt to avoid:**
```
beautiful waterfall
```
(too short, lacks detail)

### Example 2: A person
**A good Prompt:**
```
A young woman with long dark hair walking through 
a cherry blossom park in Tokyo, spring afternoon, 
soft pink petals falling, medium shot, film grain
```

---

## Negative Prompt — tell the AI what you don't want

A **Negative Prompt** (a description of what you don't want in the video) helps prevent the AI from creating unwanted things.

Example Negative Prompt:
```
blurry, distorted, low quality, watermark, text overlay, 
unrealistic proportions, extra limbs
```

## Gen-4 Turbo — the Credit-saving option

If you want to experiment and don't want to spend many Credits, use **Gen-4 Turbo** (a fast, resource-economical version) instead.

- About 2× faster than Gen-4.5
- Uses only 5 credits/second (compared to 12 credits/second for Gen-4.5)
- Quality is still very good, good for draft work or testing concepts

---

## Limitations and things to know

- **Runway can't create** violent imagery, adult content, or copyright-infringing content
- **Real people's faces** may be filtered if they don't pass the Content Moderation policy
- **Text in the video** may still be hard to read or garbled (the AI still can't render characters perfectly)
- Credits spent aren't refunded even if you don't like the result

---

## Summary

Gen-4.5 is Runway's most powerful video model at present. The key is writing a Prompt with enough detail — the better the Prompt, the more beautiful the result. Practice writing several kinds of Prompts and compare the results to find the formula that suits your work.
