# Gemini Advanced — premium features and the Google One AI Premium plan

**Gemini Advanced** is Gemini's premium service level that comes with the **Google One AI Premium** plan, giving users access to Gemini's most powerful models (the AI brain) plus several advanced features not in the free version.

---

## The difference between Gemini Free vs Gemini Advanced

| Feature | Gemini Free | Gemini Advanced |
|---|---|---|
| AI model | Gemini 2.0 Flash | Gemini 2.5 Pro / Ultra |
| Deep Research | Limited number of times | Unlimited (higher volume) |
| Gems (custom AI) | Limited | Create unlimited |
| Canvas (a space to write and create work) | Basic | Full features, including building apps |
| Context Window (the amount of data the AI takes in at once) | 1 million tokens | 2 million tokens+ |
| Google One Storage | Free plan (15 GB) | 2 TB or more |
| Gemini in Workspace | Not included | Included for personal |
| Upload large files | Limited | Larger |

---

## Models available in Gemini Advanced

Gemini Advanced gives access to Google's top-tier models (the AI):

### Gemini 2.5 Pro
- The smartest model for complex work
- Good for: coding, in-depth analysis, answering complex questions
- Supports a very large context window
- Has **Thinking Mode** (a deep-thinking mode — the AI thinks step by step before answering, also called Deep Think) for step-by-step reasoning

### Gemini 2.5 Flash
- Faster, more economical
- Still highly intelligent
- Good for general work that needs speed

---

## Standout features of Gemini Advanced

### 1. Unlimited Deep Research
- Research the web deeply and create reports automatically
- Advanced users can do it more often and get more comprehensive reports

### 2. More Gems (personal AI assistants)
- Create personal AI assistants with a persona, instructions, and behavior you define
- Keep conversation context between sessions

### 3. A large context window
- The Pro-level model supports more tokens (small pieces of text, about 1 word each)
- Analyze documents, a codebase (all the code in a project), or long videos in one go

### 4. Full-featured Canvas
- Create documents, slides, and **web applications** fully
- Edit collaboratively (together in real time)

### 5. Gemini in Google Workspace (for personal accounts)
- Use Gemini to help write in Google Docs
- Summarize emails in Gmail
- Analyze data in Google Sheets
- Create slides in Google Slides

## Plan and pricing

The **Google One AI Premium** plan includes:
- Gemini Advanced (the top models)
- 2 TB of Google One storage
- Gemini in Gmail, Docs, Sheets, Slides (personal accounts)
- Extra Google Photos AI features
- Magic Eraser, Photo Unblur, and AI tools in Google Photos

> Price and plan details may differ by country. Check at [one.google.com](https://one.google.com)

---

## How to subscribe to Gemini Advanced

1. Open [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com) or the Gemini app
2. Click your profile picture, or choose **Upgrade** in the menu
3. Choose the **Google One AI Premium** plan
4. Pay through your Google account
5. Gemini Advanced is enabled immediately

---

## When should you upgrade?

**Upgrade when you need:**
- Research or large writing projects often
- Coding or fixing complex bugs (errors in a program)
- Analyzing long, multi-page documents
- Using Gemini to help with personal Workspace
- 2 TB of Google One storage

**The free version is enough if:**
- You use it generally, for Q&A and summaries
- You don't use it often
- You only need basic features

---

## Tips for using Gemini Advanced

- **Choose the model to suit the work** — use 2.5 Pro for complex work, Flash for fast work
- **Use Deep Think for hard problems** — turn on the "Think deeper" option so the model thinks carefully before answering
- **Create Gems for routine work** — define the persona and frequently used context in advance
- **Upload several documents at once** — make use of the large context window
