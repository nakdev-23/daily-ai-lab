# Profiles — profile and community

> Main reference: [Profiles](https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/41117938447629-Profiles)

---

## What is a Profile

A Profile (your public identity page in the Midjourney community) shows the work you've created to others and lets you follow other creators.

---

## Components of a Profile

### Personal information
- Profile picture
- Username
- Bio (a short biography)

### Public Portfolio
- Shows the work you've set to "Public"
- Others can Like and Follow

### Your work
- Images you've created and chosen to share publicly

---

## Visibility settings

Control which work is seen publicly:

- **Public** — work appears in Explore and the Profile
- **Private** (requires Stealth Mode) — work doesn't appear publicly

### Change a work's Visibility
1. Click an image in the Archive
2. Choose "Make Public" or "Make Private"

---

## Following and Exploring

### Follow a creator
- Go to Explore → click a piece of work
- Click the creator's name → "Follow"
- New work from those you follow appears in your Feed

### Discover
- Use Explore to find creators with a style you like
- Find inspiration from the Prompts of work you like

---

## Sharing work through your Profile

### Share the Profile link
- Go to Profile → Copy Link
- Share with clients or friends to see your Portfolio

### Share individual pieces
- Click an image → "Share"
- get a direct link to that image

---

## Digital Portfolio

A Profile in Midjourney can be used as a digital Portfolio (a collection of work showing your abilities):

- Choose your best work to be Public
- Work is sorted by most recent date
- Viewers can Like your work

### Tips for building a Portfolio
1. Choose only your best work to be Public
2. Cover several Styles to show variety
3. Update regularly with new work

---

## Stats and Analytics

In your Profile you can see:
- The number of Likes received
- The number of Followers
- Your most popular work

---

## Summary

A Profile lets you be part of the Midjourney community, share work, follow other creators, and build a public digital Portfolio. Set the Visibility to suit your privacy needs, and use the Profile as a channel to show your work to clients or colleagues.
