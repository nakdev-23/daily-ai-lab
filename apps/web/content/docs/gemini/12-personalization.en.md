# 12 — Personalization — tune Gemini to know you

Reference: [Get Personalization](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16598623) | [Memory](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16598469) | [Custom Instructions](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16598625)

---

## What is Personalization

**Personalization** is making Gemini adapt specifically to you instead of answering everyone the same way. When Personalization is on, Gemini will:
- Remember things you mentioned in earlier chats
- Adjust the tone and format of answers to suit you
- Use data from connected apps in its answers

---

## 3 forms of Personalization

### 1. Memory — remember from past chats

Gemini can remember information from earlier conversations and use it in new ones.

**Example:** If you once said you like vegetarian food, the next time you ask about recipes, Gemini suggests meatless recipes automatically.

**How to turn Memory on/off:**
1. Menu (☰) → **Settings** → **Personal intelligence** → **Memory**
2. Turn it on or off as you like

**View and delete memories:**
- In Settings → Memory, you'll see the list of what Gemini remembers about you
- Click to delete anything you don't want

> Currently supported only in English in the United States.

Reference: [Memory](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16598469)

### 2. Custom Instructions — tell it in advance what you want

**Custom Instructions** is telling Gemini in advance how you want it to answer, every time, without retyping.

**Examples to put in Custom Instructions:**
```
- I'm an intermediate Python developer
- I like concise answers, always with code examples
- Explain in Thai, but use English for technical terms
- If I ask about food, suggest only vegetarian food
```

**How to set Custom Instructions:**
1. Menu (☰) → **Settings** → **Custom instructions** (or Personalization)
2. Type the instructions you want
3. Save

Reference: [Custom Instructions](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16598625)

### 3. Connected Apps Personalization — data from connected apps

Gemini uses data from Gmail, Calendar, Drive, etc. to give answers relevant to your real life.

**Examples:**
- Ask "What appointments do I have this week?" → Gemini pulls from Calendar instantly
- Ask "Summarize the important emails this week" → Gemini reads Gmail for you

Reference: [About Connected Apps Personalization](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16836988)

---

## How to turn on all Personalization

**Conditions you need:**
- Logged in with a personal account
- Turn on **Keep Activity**
- Connect the Connected Apps you want

**Steps to turn it on:**
1. Menu (☰) → **Settings** → **Personal intelligence**
2. Turn on Personalization (Memory, Connected Apps, Custom Instructions)

---

## Privacy in Personalization

- The data Gemini "remembers" is stored in Gemini Apps Activity
- You can view, edit, or delete memory data anytime
- You can turn off all Personalization in Settings

---

## Personalization with a work/school account

For work/school accounts, Personalization may have limits per the organization's policy. The administrator can turn this feature on or off.

---

## Summary

Personalization makes Gemini know you better and answer more on point. Memory (remembering from old chats), Custom Instructions (telling it in advance), and Connected Apps (data from real apps) work together to give you the most fitting answers.
