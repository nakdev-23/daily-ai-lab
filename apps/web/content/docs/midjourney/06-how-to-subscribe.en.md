# Plans and subscription

> Main references: [How to Subscribe](https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/31974654274573-How-to-Subscribe), [Comparing Midjourney Plans](https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/27870484040333-Comparing-Midjourney-Plans)

## Overview

Midjourney has no free plan (it was discontinued). You must subscribe to a paid membership to use it. There are plans to choose from based on your needs and budget.

## Understanding GPU Time

### Fast Mode
- GPU Fast Time (high-speed image-processing time — uses a good GPU to create images quickly) is the main resource used to create images
- Creating an image once uses about 1-2 minutes of GPU Time
- When it runs out, you must buy more or wait for the new monthly cycle

### Relax Mode
- Usable unlimited on the Standard plan and up
- Creates images slower (may wait 0-10 minutes depending on the queue)
- Doesn't count GPU Fast Time
- Good for non-urgent work

### Turbo Mode
- Up to 4 times faster than Fast Mode
- Uses 2 times the GPU Time
- Good for urgent work

## How to subscribe

### Through the website
1. Go to [midjourney.com/account](https://midjourney.com/account) or [midjourney.com/subscribe](https://midjourney.com/subscribe)
2. Click **"Subscribe"**
3. Choose the plan you want
4. Enter credit card information (supports Visa, Mastercard, American Express)
5. Choose to pay monthly or annually
6. Confirm the subscription

### Through Discord
1. Type `/subscribe` in Discord
2. The Midjourney Bot sends you a subscription link
3. Click the link to go to the payment page

## Which plan suits you

### Basic plan ($10/month) — good for:
- Beginners who want to try it out
- Light use, no more than 200 images/month
- No need for Relax Mode or Stealth Mode

### Standard plan ($30/month) — good for:
- General users who create images regularly
- Want Relax Mode for unlimited image creation
- Creating images for Creative work or Social Media

### Pro plan ($60/month) — good for:
- Professional designers and artists
- Want Stealth Mode (maximum privacy)
- Heavy use needing lots of Fast Time

### Mega plan ($120/month) — good for:
- Businesses and creative teams
- Need a large amount of GPU Time every month
- Enterprise-level image production

## Managing your membership

### Cancel the membership
- Go to [midjourney.com/account](https://midjourney.com/account)
- Click **"Manage"** under the current plan
- Choose **"Cancel Plan"**
- Your access remains until the end of the billing cycle

### Upgrade/downgrade
- Doable at any time from the Account page
- Upgrade — start using the new plan immediately (charged the difference)
- Downgrade — start using the new plan in the next cycle

### Buying more Fast Time
- Buy more Fast Time at $4 per 1 hour
- No expiration (it stays in your account across months)

---

## Important information

- **Auto-renews** every month/year (can be turned off in Settings)
- **GPU Fast Time doesn't accumulate across months** — if unused, it disappears
- **Purchased Fast Time doesn't expire** — it stays until used up
- **Payment via Stripe** (a trusted online payment-processing company)

## Frequently asked questions

**Q: Is there a free trial?**
A: Midjourney currently no longer has a free trial period; you must subscribe to a paid plan.

**Q: If I cancel, will I lose my images?**
A: No. All images stay in your account and are downloadable, but you can't create new images.

**Q: Can I use images commercially?**
A: Yes, for paid plans. See the details in the Terms of Service topic.

## Summary

Start with the **Standard ($30)** plan if you want unlimited Relax Mode, or **Basic ($10)** if you want to try first. The Pro and Mega plans are good for professionals who need Stealth Mode and a lot of Fast Time.
