# Voices & Voice Cloning — choose and clone voices 🎭

> Adapted from the official documentation at [elevenlabs.io/docs](https://elevenlabs.io/docs), the Voices section

ElevenLabs lets you **choose a ready-made voice** from the library or **create a unique voice** by cloning a voice.

## 🗂️ Types of voices

| Type | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Voice Library** | A library of thousands of ready-made voices to choose from |
| **Instant Voice Clone** | A fast clone from a short audio sample |
| **Professional Voice Clone** | A high-quality clone from a long audio sample (very accurate) |
| **Voice Design** | Create a new voice from a description of the voice's characteristics |

## ⚡ Instant vs Professional

- **Instant** — uses a few minutes of audio sample, gives instant results, good for playing around / general work
- **Professional** — needs a longer recorded sample (and takes time to train the model) but gives the most faithful voice, good for serious work

## ⚠️ Important: consent

Voice cloning must be **your own voice** or a voice with **explicit permission** only — ElevenLabs has a verification system and terms to prevent using someone else's voice without permission.

## ▶️ How to clone a voice (Instant)

1. Go to **Voices → Add a new voice → Instant Voice Clone**
2. Upload/record an audio sample (clear audio, no background noise)
3. Confirm you have the right to use this voice
4. Name it, then use it on the Text to Speech page

> 💡 A good-quality audio sample (quiet, no echo, clear speech) = a better cloned voice
