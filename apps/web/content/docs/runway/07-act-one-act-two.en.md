# Act-One and Act-Two — AI character animation

> **Act-One** and **Act-Two** are technologies that make an AI character move according to a real actor's performance — like Motion Capture but much easier.

---

## What is Act-One?

**Act-One** (the first-generation character animation system) is a tool that transfers Facial Expressions from a real actor's video to an AI character or cartoon character.

**Main capabilities:**
- Transfer facial expressions from a person to a character
- Supports both Realistic and Cartoon characters
- Works with just one character image, no 3D model needed

## What is Act-Two?

**Act-Two** (the second-generation character animation system) is the version developed from Act-One, adding the ability to transfer Full Body Performance, not just the face.

**Additional capabilities:**
- Full body motion transfer
- Gestures, hands, torso, and walking
- Good for complex Character animation

---

## How to use Act-One

### Step 1: Prepare the actor video (Driver Video)

A **Driver Video** (a performance source video — a video of a real actor performing the motion) is a video of yourself or an actor who will transfer the expression.

Driver Video requirements:
- **The face must be clearly visible** and centered in the frame
- **Good lighting** with no shadows blocking the face
- **No mask** or anything covering the face
- Recommended length: 5-30 seconds
- Should not have frequent cuts

**Tips for shooting a Driver Video:**
```
- Shoot with the front camera at a straight angle
- Light from the front, or a Ring light
- A simple background
- Speak and express clearly, squint, smile
```

### Step 2: Prepare the Character Image

A **Character Image** is an image of the character that will receive the expression.

Character Image requirements:
- The face facing straight or slightly tilted
- A clear face with visible features
- Supports real people, cartoons, Illustrations

### Step 3: Use it

1. Go to **"Act-One"** in the tools menu
2. Upload the **Driver Video**
3. Upload the **Character Image**
4. Click **"Generate"**

The AI transfers the expression from the Driver Video to the character in the Character Image.

---

## How to use Act-Two

### Step 1: Prepare a Performance Video

The **Performance Video** for Act-Two must show the full body, or at least the upper half.

Requirements:
- The body clearly visible, not obstructed
- Consistent lighting
- A fast enough Shutter speed so the image isn't blurry
- Walking, running, or performing the gesture you want

### Step 2: Character Reference

For Act-Two, use a character image showing the full body or at least the upper half.

### Step 3: Generate

The same process as Act-One, but the result includes body motion.

## Limitations and Content Policy

- **Can't use** real people's faces without permission
- **No creating Deepfakes** (fake videos using a real person's face without consent)
- You must have rights to the Driver video used
- Runway's Content Moderation system filters inappropriate content

## Summary

Act-One and Act-Two completely change how animation is made. Where it once required a team of Animators and expensive 3D software, now anyone can create lively characters by filming a video of themselves for just a few seconds. It's good for both animation professionals and general content creators.
