## Gemini Apps Privacy Hub

The **Privacy Hub** is a central place for information about how Gemini stores and uses your data.

Reference: [Privacy Hub](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13594961)

---

## Data Gemini stores

### Gemini Apps Activity

Gemini records **Gemini Apps Activity**, which includes:
- The chats and prompts you send
- The answers Gemini gives
- The files you upload

**Keep Activity** is turning on Gemini's saving of Activity:
- If on → chats are saved and accessible later; Gemini may use this data to improve the service
- If off → chats aren't saved, chat history disappears, and many features won't work

### Data from Gemini Live

Voice conversations in Gemini Live are processed, but the audio isn't stored permanently on Google's servers — only the text transcript is kept.

---

## How to manage Gemini Apps Activity

### View activity
1. Go to [myactivity.google.com/product/gemini](https://myactivity.google.com/product/gemini)
2. Or go to gemini.google.com → Settings & help → Activity

### Delete activity
1. Go to Activity
2. Choose the chats or time range to delete
3. Click **Delete**

**Delete options:**
- Delete chat by chat
- Delete by time range (e.g. the last 7 days, 30 days, all)
- Set auto-delete every 3, 18, or 36 months

## Gemini and data in Google Workspace

When you connect Google Workspace (Gmail, Drive, etc.), Gemini accesses that data to answer your questions, but:
- Google won't use data in Gmail or Drive to train AI models without permission
- See the detailed policy at [Privacy Hub](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13594961#data_exchange)

## Download your Gemini data

You can download all the data Gemini stores about you:

1. Go to [Google Takeout](https://takeout.google.com)
2. Choose **Gemini Apps**
3. Download it as a ZIP file

Reference: [Download Your Gemini Data](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16920332)

## Canvas Safety

Content created in Canvas and publicly shared apps has extra safety policies, e.g.:
- Preventing prompt injection in created apps
- Checking content before displaying it

Reference: [Canvas Safety](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16419134)

## SynthID — verify AI-created media

**SynthID** is a watermark invisible to the naked eye, embedded in images, video, audio, and text created by Google's AI.

**Why it matters:**
- Helps verify whether media was really created by AI
- Reduces the spread of deepfakes and fake media
- Use the SynthID verification tool at: [SynthID Detector](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16722517)

---

## Prompt Injection protection

Gemini has prompt-injection protection — for cases where a website or document has hidden commands to trick Gemini:

- Gemini tries to detect and not follow hidden commands
- But the system isn't 100% perfect — so supervise closely when using Gemini Agent with external websites

Reference: [Prompt Injection Protection](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16188217)

---

## Generative AI Prohibited Use Policy

Gemini has a policy forbidding certain uses, e.g.:
- Creating harmful or illegal content
- Infringing copyright or someone's privacy
- Creating false information to deceive
- Creating sexual content involving minors

If you find a violation: [Report a Violation](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16719589)

Reference: [Prohibited Use Policy](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16625148)

## Use by children and parents

| Age | Ability |
|---|---|
| Under 13 | Can't use Gemini (needs a Family Link account) |
| 13–17 | Can use Gemini, but some features are limited |
| 18+ | Full use |

**Parents can:**
- Turn off access to Gemini for children in a Family Link account
- Review and manage children's activity

Reference: [Guide Your Child's Gemini Experience](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16109150)

---

## Summary

Gemini emphasizes privacy by giving you full control of your own data — view, edit, delete, or download it anytime. It also has SynthID, prompt-injection protection, and clear policies for using AI responsibly.
