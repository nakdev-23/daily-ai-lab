# Prompt-writing techniques for Seedance ✍️

> Compiled in English from Seedance's Prompt guide (ByteDance / BytePlus ModelArk)

How good the video turns out depends mainly on the prompt. This page collects structures that actually work.

## 🧩 Recommended Prompt structure

Ordering it roughly like this helps the model understand easily:

1. **Subject** — who/what, e.g. "a young woman in a red dress"
2. **Action** — what they're doing, e.g. "strolling along the sea"
3. **Scene** — where, what time, e.g. "a beach at sunset"
4. **Camera** — angle/motion, e.g. "the camera following from behind, panning slowly"
5. **Style/light** — e.g. "film tone, golden light, realistic"

## ✅ Do

- Be specific and concrete (state colors, light, mood)
- Specify the camera motion clearly
- Use visual description, not abstract commands

## ❌ Avoid

- Prompts that are too short/broad, e.g. "a nice video"
- Cramming several scenes/events into one clip
- Self-contradictory words (e.g. "daytime" + "starlight")

## 🎯 Comparison example

| Broad (bad) | Specific (good) |
|---|---|
| "a cute cat" | "an orange kitten hopping around on a sofa, soft light from the window, close-up camera, slow motion" |
