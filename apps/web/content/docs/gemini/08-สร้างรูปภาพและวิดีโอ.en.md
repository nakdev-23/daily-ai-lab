## Create images with Imagen (Nano Banana 2)

### What is Nano Banana 2

**Nano Banana 2** is Google's AI image-generation model integrated into Gemini Apps, used to create and edit images from a text prompt.

**Nano Banana 2's standout abilities:**
- **World Knowledge** — draw diagrams and infographics with accurate information
- **Character Consistency** — keep a character's appearance the same across several images
- **Local Edits** — edit only part of an image
- **Text Rendering** — put text in images accurately, in any language
- **Complex Instructions** — understand complex commands and capture details
- **High Resolution** — download at 2K (AI plans) or 1K (free)

### How to create an image

1. Go to [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com)
2. Click **Add Files** → **Create image**
3. Type a prompt describing the image you want
4. Click Submit

### Tips for writing image prompts

- **Start with a verb:** "Draw", "Generate", "Create", "Make"
- **Specify the style:** photorealistic, watercolor, charcoal drawing, cartoon, anime, oil painting
- **Describe the details:** what's in the image, what the characters are doing, the background

**Examples:**
```
"Generate an image of a futuristic car driving through an old mountain road surrounded by nature"

"Create a watercolor painting of a golden sunset over the ocean with silhouette of coconut trees"

"Draw a cute cartoon dog riding a surfboard in Hawaii"

"Create an infographic showing the steps of planting a tree"
```

### Edit images

You can edit a created image, or upload your own image for Gemini to edit:

**Example image-edit commands:**
```
"Change the background color to blue"

"Add red flowers in the bottom-left corner"

"Make this image look anime style"

"Upload a photo of yourself and ask 'put me in a beach scene, watercolor style'"
```

### Redo with Nano Banana Pro (paid plans)

Users with a paid plan can redo an image with Nano Banana Pro, which gives higher detail, especially for images with text or infographics:
1. Create an image with Nano Banana 2
2. Click **More** (⋮) → **Redo with Pro**

### Download and export images

- **Download:** hover over the image → click **Download full size**
- **Export to Docs:** click **More** (⋮) → **Export to Docs**

### Image creation limits

| Plan | Images/day |
|---|---|
| Free | 20 images |
| Google AI Plus | 50 images |
| Google AI Pro | 100 images |
| Google AI Ultra | 1,000 images |

> Limits may change often due to high demand.

## Create video with Veo

**Veo** is Google's AI video-generation model integrated into Gemini Apps.

| Plan | Ability |
|---|---|

| **Google AI Plus** | Veo 3.1 Fast (Preview) — 2 videos/day |
| **Google AI Pro** | Veo 3.1 Fast (Preview) — 3 videos/day |
| **Google AI Ultra** | Veo 3.1 (Preview) — 5 videos/day |

## Create music with Gemini

Gemini can create both short clips (30 sec) and full songs (up to 3 minutes).

| Plan | 30-sec songs/day | Full songs/day |
|---|---|---|
| **Free** | 10 tracks | Not supported |
| **Google AI Plus** | 20 tracks | 10 tracks |
| **Google AI Pro** | 50 tracks | 20 tracks |
| **Google AI Ultra** | 100 tracks | 50 tracks |

Reference: [Generate Music](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16901237)

## Audio Overviews — listen to a podcast-style summary

**Audio Overviews** turn content into a podcast-style audio conversation between 2 people, good for people who like learning by listening.

**Can be made from:**
- A conversation or report in Gemini
- An uploaded file
- A Deep Research report

**How to create an Audio Overview:**
1. Open the chat or report you want
2. In the Canvas panel on the right, click **Create** → **Audio Overview**

**Limit:** 20 Audio Overviews per day (all plans)

Reference: [Audio Overviews](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16047373)

## SynthID — verify AI-created media

Images, videos, and audio created by Gemini have **SynthID** embedded — a watermark invisible to the naked eye, used to verify the media was created by Google's AI.

Verification tool: [SynthID Detector](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16722517)
