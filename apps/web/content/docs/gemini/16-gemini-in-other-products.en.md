# 16 — Gemini in other Google products

Reference: [Gemini in Chrome](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/14886647) | [Gemini in Messages](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/14599070) | [Gemini in Car](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16735982)

---

## Gemini in Google Chrome

Gemini is integrated into Chrome to help while you browse.

**What you can do:**
- Ask questions about the web page you're viewing
- Summarize article content
- Help find information on the page

**How to turn on Gemini in Chrome:**
- In Chrome, click the Gemini icon in the toolbar (if available)
- Or press Ctrl+Shift+Space (Windows) / Cmd+Shift+Space (Mac)

Reference: [Gemini in Chrome](https://support.google.com/gemini/topic/16695153)

---

## Gemini in Google Messages

Gemini is available in Google Messages on Android, like chatting with a contact on your phone.

**What you can do:**
- Ask questions, find information
- Help write reply messages
- Brainstorm, plan

**How to turn it on:**
1. Open the Google Messages app
2. Tap the Gemini icon or search "Gemini" in the chat list

> **Limit:** Gemini in Messages doesn't support advanced features like Gems or Connected Apps.

Reference: [Gemini in Messages](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/14599070)

---

## Gemini in cars

Gemini is available in Google-supported cars via Android Auto or Google built-in.

**What you can do:**
- Ask questions by voice while driving
- Navigate, find places
- Play music, manage calls

Reference: [Gemini in Car](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16735982)

---

## Gemini Live on Android XR

For Android XR users (Google's AR/VR glasses):
- Use Gemini Live hands-free
- See answers overlaid on the real world
- Combine AI with an augmented-reality experience

Reference: [Gemini on Android XR](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16665938)

---

## Gemini in Workspace (for organizations)

For work-account users, Gemini is integrated into Google Workspace:
- **Gmail** — help write and summarize emails
- **Google Docs** — help write and edit documents
- **Google Slides** — create slides from an outline
- **Google Sheets** — analyze data, create formulas
- **Google Meet** — summarize meetings

> Workspace features require permission from the administrator.

---

## Gemini in Search

Google Search includes Gemini AI in the form of **AI Overviews** — a summarized answer at the top of search results, good for questions needing a long answer or steps.

---

## Gemini in Google Photos

When you connect Google Photos with Gemini:
- Find images with natural language ("photos from last year's birthday")
- Analyze images
- Create captions or stories from photos

---

## Gemini in Google Maps (automatic)

Gemini pulls public data from Google Maps to use in Live chats and general conversation automatically, without connecting separately.

---

## Summary

Gemini isn't just at gemini.google.com — it's embedded across Google's entire ecosystem, from Chrome, Messages, Workspace, Search, Maps, and Photos to cars and Android XR, making Gemini an AI assistant everywhere in daily life.
