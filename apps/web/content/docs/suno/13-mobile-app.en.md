# Suno Mobile App

Suno has a mobile app for both iOS (iPhone/iPad) and Android, letting you create songs anywhere, anytime.

---

## Download the app

### iOS (iPhone / iPad)

1. Open the **App Store** on iPhone or iPad
2. Search for **"Suno - Make Music"**
3. Download for free (using your configured Apple ID)
4. Or click the direct link: [App Store - Suno](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/suno-make-music/id6480136315)

### Android

1. Open the **Google Play Store** on your Android phone
2. Search for **"Suno - AI Music"**
3. Download for free
4. Or click the direct link: [Google Play - Suno](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.suno.android)

## Log In

### How to log in on the app

1. Open the Suno app
2. Tap **"Sign In"**
3. Choose a login method:
   - **Google** (recommended)
   - **Apple ID** (iOS only)
   - **Discord**
   - **Email + Password**

> **Tip:** Use the same login method you use on the web, so you access the same songs and Credits.

### Common login problems

| Problem | How to fix |
|------|-------|
| Can't log in with phone number | use Google or Email instead |
| Forgot password | press "Forgot Password" and reset via email |
| Account is Locked | contact Suno Support |
| The app won't accept Google Login | clear the Cache and try again |

## The app's sections

### Home

- Shows recommended songs and Trending (popular songs)
- Songs from Creators you Follow
- A shortcut to Create

### Create

- Works the same as the web version
- Full Simple Mode and Custom Mode
- Supports Audio Upload from mobile

### Library

**Library** (where all the songs you create are stored) shows:

- All the songs you've created
- Filters by: recent, most liked, Public/Private
- Download, share, or delete songs directly

### Explore

**Explore** (the page showing songs from users worldwide) lets you:

- Find songs by Genre or Keyword
- See Trending Songs
- Discover new Creators
- Like, Comment, or Remix songs you like

## Mobile-only features

### Suno Hooks

**Hooks** (a feature combining songs and short videos to share on Social Media) is a standout feature on mobile:

- Create a Short-form Video to accompany a song
- Add Visuals (animation) to the song
- Share to TikTok, Instagram Reels, or YouTube Shorts

### How to create a Hook

1. Open the song you want
2. Tap **"Create Hook"** or **"Hooks"**
3. Choose a Visual Style for the video
4. Specify the part of the song to show (e.g. the Chorus)
5. Save and share

## Managing your account in the app

### Delete Account

> **Warning:** Deleting your account permanently deletes all your songs and cannot be recovered.

1. Go to **Profile**
2. Tap **Settings**
3. Scroll down to **"Delete Account"**
4. Read the warning and confirm

### Cancel a Subscription through the app

#### iOS (cancel through the App Store)

Canceling a Subscription on iOS is done through App Store Settings:

1. Open the iPhone's **Settings**
2. Tap your Apple ID name at the top
3. Choose **"Subscriptions"**
4. Find **Suno** in the list
5. Tap **"Cancel Subscription"**

> **Note:** You can't cancel directly through the Suno app on iOS — you must do it through Apple.

#### Android (cancel through Google Play)

1. Open the **Google Play Store**
2. Tap the Profile icon in the top-right corner
3. Choose **"Payments & subscriptions"**
4. Choose **"Subscriptions"**
5. Find **Suno** and tap **"Cancel"**
