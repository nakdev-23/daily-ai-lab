# 14 — Gemini Apps settings

Reference: [Dark Theme](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13542227) | [Change Voice](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/15277943) | [Change Language](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/15984485)

---

## How to open the settings page

**On the web app:**
1. Go to gemini.google.com
2. Click the menu (☰) on the left → **Settings & help**

**On mobile:**
1. Open the Gemini app
2. Menu (☰) → profile picture → **Settings**

---

## Dark Theme

Make Gemini display in dark mode, reducing eye strain in low light.

**How to turn on Dark Theme:**
1. Settings & help → **Dark theme**
2. Choose: Light, Dark, or Use device theme

Reference: [Dark Theme](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13542227)

---

## Change Gemini's voice

**Gemini Voice** is used in features where Gemini speaks aloud, e.g. Gemini Live and the Listen button on answers.

**How to change the voice (mobile):**
1. Menu (☰) → profile picture → **Settings** → **Gemini's Voice**
2. Tap to listen to each voice
3. Click **Confirm**

> Voice options may not be available in some languages.

Reference: [Change Voice](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/15277943)

---

## Change language

**The language used in Gemini** sets both the UI and the caption language in Gemini Live.

**How to change the language:**
1. Settings & help → **Language**
2. Choose the language you want

> Gemini supports Thai, plus over 40 other languages.

Reference: [Change Language](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/15984485)

---

## Visual Layout and Dynamic View

**Dynamic View** adjusts how answers are displayed to be nicer and easier to read.

**How to turn it on:**
- Dynamic View usually shows automatically for some answers
- You can toggle it in Settings

Reference: [Visual Layout](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16741341)

---

## Notebooks — notebooks in Gemini

**Notebooks** are a space to store and organize chats, documents, and important data, similar to a notebook in NotebookLM.

**How to create a Notebook:**
1. Menu (☰) → **Notebooks** → **+ New notebook**
2. Add the chats or content you want to keep

Reference: [Notebooks in Gemini](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16972047)

---

## Import from another AI platform

Gemini supports importing data from other AI platforms, e.g. ChatGPT.

**How to import:**
1. Settings → **Import from another AI**
2. Follow the given steps

Reference: [Import from other AI platforms](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16868299)

---

## Settings for a work/school account

Some settings in a work/school account are controlled by the administrator (admin):
- The admin may turn some features off/on
- Activity may be managed by the organization

Admins can find more at: [Gemini for Workspace Admin](https://support.google.com/a/answer/14571493)

---

## Send feedback and report problems

If you find a problem or want to give feedback:

1. In a Gemini answer, click **Thumbs down** (👎) to mark the answer as poor
2. Or go to Settings → **Send feedback**
3. Or go to: [Report a Problem](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13275746)

---

## Summary of all setting options

| Setting | Details |
|---|---|
| Dark Theme | Toggle dark/light mode |
| Gemini's Voice | Change Gemini's speaking voice |
| Language | Change the UI and caption language |
| Custom Instructions | Special instructions for Gemini |
| Memory | Turn remembering from old chats on/off |
| Connected Apps | Connect/disconnect apps |
| Activity | View and delete chat history |
| Scheduled Actions | Manage automated work |
| Remote Browser Data | Delete the Gemini Agent's browser data |
| Notebooks | Notebooks in Gemini |
