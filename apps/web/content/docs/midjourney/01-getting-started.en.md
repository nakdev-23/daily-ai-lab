# Getting started with Midjourney

> Main reference: [Midjourney Getting Started Guide](https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/33329261836941-Getting-Started-Guide)

---

## What is Midjourney

Midjourney is an AI (artificial intelligence — a computer system that learns and can create work like a human) image-creation service that lets you type a text description, and the system automatically creates an image matching what you want.

For example, if you type "a cat sitting on a mountain at sunset, watercolor style," the system instantly creates an image of a cat sitting on a mountain at sunset in a watercolor style.

### Why Midjourney is popular

- **High quality** — the resulting images are professionally beautiful
- **Easy to use** — just type a description (Prompt) and get an image instantly
- **Versatility** — create any style, from paintings to realistic photos to fantasy art
- **A creative community** — millions of users worldwide share their work

## Getting-started steps

### Step 1 — Sign up
1. Open a browser and go to [midjourney.com](https://midjourney.com)
2. Click **"Sign In"** or **"Get Started"**
3. Choose a sign-up method: use a Google account or email
4. Choose the Plan (membership level) you want

### Step 2 — Choose the right plan

| Plan | Price/month | GPU Fast (high-speed image-creation hours) | Good for |
|-----|-----------|--------------------------------------|------------|
| Basic | $10 | 3.3 hours | beginners, light use |
| Standard | $30 | 15 hours | general users |
| Pro | $60 | 30 hours | professional creators |
| Mega | $120 | 60 hours | business use |

### Step 3 — Create your first image
1. Go to midjourney.com and log in
2. Click the image-creation box or the **"Create"** button
3. Type a Prompt (the command you type for the AI to create an image — like telling it what kind of image you want) in English, e.g. `a cozy coffee shop interior, warm lighting, photorealistic`
4. Press Enter and wait about 1 minute
5. The system creates 4 options at once

## Understanding the website Interface

Once logged in, you'll see these sections:

- **Explore** — see all the community's work, good for finding inspiration
- **Create** — your image-creation area
- **Archive** — the gallery of all the images you've created
- **Settings** — various account settings

---

## Tips for beginners

**1. Start with a simple Prompt**
You don't have to type a long one from the start. Try starting with short words like `sunset over the ocean`, then gradually add detail.

**2. Use English**
Midjourney understands English best. Although it accepts some Thai, the results may not be as accurate.

**3. Look at others' work for inspiration**
Go to the Explore page and look at the Prompts of work you like, to learn how to write a good Prompt.

**4. You can always try again**
Midjourney creates different results each time. If you don't like it, try creating again or adjusting the Prompt.
