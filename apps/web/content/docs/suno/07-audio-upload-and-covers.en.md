# Audio Upload and Cover Songs

Suno doesn't only work with text — it can also take **Audio Input** from you, then transform it or use it as inspiration to create a new song.

---

## What is Upload Audio?

**Upload Audio** (bringing your own audio file into Suno) lets you:

- Hum a tune (Humming — singing without lyrics) and have Suno turn it into a real song
- Upload a Reference audio so Suno makes a song in the same style
- Use the original audio as the Base of a new song

---

## How to use Upload Audio

### Steps

1. Go to the **Create** page
2. Click the **Upload** icon (an up-arrow) or the **"Upload Audio"** button
3. Choose an audio file from your computer
4. Wait for Suno to process the file
5. Enter additional instructions for what you want done with that audio
6. Click **"Create"**

### Supported file types

- **MP3** — the most common audio file
- **WAV** — high quality (Lossless — no quality loss)
- **M4A** — Apple's audio file
- **OGG** — an Open Source audio file

---

## What you can do with Upload Audio

### 1. Hum to Song

Hum the melody in your head and record it, then upload it for Suno to create a full song.

**Steps:**
- Record yourself humming the tune you want
- Upload that audio file
- Tell Suno what Genre you want, e.g. `"Pop song with vocals based on this melody"`
- Suno composes the music and lyrics around your melody

### 2. Reference Track

Upload a song you like as a Reference so Suno creates a song in a close style.

> **Important note:** Suno doesn't copy the original song; it only learns from the "Sonic Characteristics."

### 3. Custom Sounds into a song

Have special sounds like rain, birdsong, or the sea? Upload them and have Suno compose a song using those sounds as components.

## Cover Songs

A **Cover Song** (singing or recording an existing song by another artist) in Suno means creating a new version of an existing song.

### Cover vs. Original

| Type | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Original Song** | a song Suno creates entirely new |
| **Cover Song** | a song created based on an existing song |

### How to create a Cover in Suno

**Method 1: Through the Cover button on a song in Suno**
1. Open another user's song in Suno
2. Click the **"Cover"** button
3. Suno creates a new version based on the original song

**Method 2: Upload the original song**
1. Upload the song file you want to Cover
2. Specify in the Prompt what style of Cover you want

### Examples of making a Cover

Create a Jazz-style Cover of a Pop song:
```
Jazz cover of a pop song, solo piano, intimate performance, late night lounge
```

Turn a slow song into a fun one:
```
Upbeat dance version, EDM remix feel, high energy, faster tempo
```

## Add Vocals

**Add Vocals** in v4.5 lets you add vocals into a song that's already Instrumental.

### How to use Add Vocals

1. Open the Instrumental song you want
2. Click **"Add Vocals"**
3. Specify the Lyrics or Style of the vocals
4. Suno adds the vocals

---

## Stem Cover in Studio

**Stem Cover** (transforming an audio track into a new sound in another style) in Suno Studio is an advanced feature that lets you transform one Stem into another sound, such as:

- Changing the guitar sound to a violin sound
- Changing the vocals to a flute sound
- Changing a Drum loop to tropical Percussion

---

## Rules about Cover Songs and copyright

### What you should know

1. **Cover Songs still have copyright** — even singing a new version, the melody and lyrics still belong to the original copyright owner
2. **Don't profit without permission** — selling a Cover requires a License first
3. **Distribution on Streaming** — if you want to upload a Cover to Spotify or YouTube Music, you need a Mechanical License
4. **Suno isn't responsible** — if you upload a copyrighted song without permission

### Recommendations

- You can freely create Covers of songs in the **Public Domain** (works whose copyright has expired)
- Songs composed before 1928 are usually in the Public Domain in the United States
- Check [Musopen](https://musopen.org/) or [IMSLP](https://imslp.org/) for copyright-free classical music

---

## Upload Audio tips

> **Tip:** A hummed audio should be at least 5–10 seconds long so the AI clearly understands the melody.

> **Tip:** Upload audio in a quiet place to reduce Background Noise that might confuse the AI.

> **Tip:** After uploading, specify a clear Style Prompt so the result matches what you want more closely.
