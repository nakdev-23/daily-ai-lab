# Runway Research — cutting-edge AI models and research

> Runway isn't just a Product company — they do advanced AI research that drives the future of Generative Media.

---

## Runway Research's vision

Runway Research focuses on building **"General-purpose Multimodal Simulators"** (AI that understands and creates many types of media) that can:
- Understand the real world through video
- Create realistic media of every type
- Interact with the real world in Real-time

---

## The main models from Runway Research

### GWM-1 — General World Model

**GWM-1** (the first-generation General World Model) is an AI model Runway describes as:

> "A state-of-the-art General World Model built to interact with the real world"

**Capabilities:**
- Create video + audio in Real-time
- Create a Digital Persona that can converse
- Understand Context in a Multimodal way (text, image, video)

**Use:** powers the Runway Characters API (GWM-1 Avatars)

---

### Gen-4.5 — the best video model

**Gen-4.5**, per Runway Research, is:

> "The world's best video model, featuring state-of-the-art motion quality"

**The technology behind it:**
- Diffusion Model (an AI that learns "denoising" to create images)
- Video-native Training (trained on massive amounts of real video)
- Motion-aware Architecture (an architecture that understands motion)

---

### Aleph 2.0 — a Video Editing model

**Aleph 2.0** is a model designed specifically for Video Editing and Video-to-Video.

**Highlights:**
- Preserves the motion from the Input Video
- Changes style consistently
- Works with Complex Scenes

---

### Act-Two — Character Performance

**Act-Two** is developed from Act-One, adding Full Body Motion Transfer.

**The research behind it:**
- Pose Estimation (detecting body poses)
- Motion Retargeting (transferring motion between different structures)
- Appearance Preservation (preserving the character's appearance)

## Latest research

### Autoregressive-to-Diffusion Vision Language Models (A2D)
**Published:** September 2025

**A2D** (Autoregressive-to-Diffusion) combines two architectures:
- **Autoregressive Model** (generates data one Token at a time), good for understanding language
- **Diffusion Model**, good for creating high-quality images

**Goal:** create a Vision Language Model that can both understand and create media in one model.

---

### Dual-Process Image Generation
**Published:** June 2025

Inspired by **Dual-process Theory** in psychology:
- **System 1** — fast processing, intuitive
- **System 2** — slow processing, deliberative

This research creates a **Distillation scheme** (a way to transfer knowledge) that lets an Image Generator learn new tasks faster.

---

### StochasticSplats — 3D Gaussian Splatting
**Published:** March 2025

**3D Gaussian Splatting** (a 3D rendering technique that uses fuzzy points instead of polygons) is a 3D Rendering technology that:
- Is much faster than NeRF (Neural Radiance Fields)
- Produces realistic images from photos at multiple angles

The **StochasticSplats research** solves the **Sorting artifacts** (defects from sorting — a problem that makes images look abnormal) in 3DGS with Stochastic Rasterization.

## Runway's main research topics

### 1. Video as Primary Modality
Runway sees video as the **"primary language"** of AI — not just Output but an Interface with the world.

Video has information images don't:
- Time and change
- Cause and effect (Causality)
- Physics and motion

### 2. Vision-Language Models
A **VLM** (Vision-Language Model) is AI that understands both image and text at once.

Runway researches VLMs for:
- Understanding video content
- Automatically generating video descriptions
- Controlling Generation with text

### 3. Real-time Video Generation
Real-time video generation is a big challenge, because a normal Diffusion Model takes several seconds.

Runway researches:
- **Model Distillation** — making models smaller but still good
- **Caching techniques** — reusing data to reduce computation
- **Streaming generation** — displaying frame by frame

### 4. Robot Policy Evaluation
Runway researches using World Models to evaluate robot operating policies — using video Simulation instead of real-world testing.
