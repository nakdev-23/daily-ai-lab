# Organization & user management

> Reference: [License & User Management](https://docs.x.ai/grok/management) | [Organization Management](https://docs.x.ai/grok/organization) | [FAQ - Team Management](https://docs.x.ai/grok/faq/team-management)

## Overview

Businesses using **Grok Business** or **Enterprise** get an organization-management system to control user permissions, Licenses, and the team Workspace.

All management is done through [console.x.ai](https://console.x.ai)

---

## License & User Management

Reference: [License & User Management](https://docs.x.ai/grok/management)

### License types

| License | Features |
|---|---|
| **SuperGrok** | Full permissions, high quota |
| **SuperGrok Heavy** | For organizations that upgrade, maximum performance |

### How to assign a License

1. Go to [console.x.ai](https://console.x.ai)
2. Choose **"Assign license"**
3. Pick the user and License type
4. The user can enter the Team Workspace on grok.com immediately

### Managing members

- **Add a member:** the Admin invites by email on the Console page
- **Remove a member:** revoke the License from the Console
- **Members without a License:** can't enter the Team Workspace and can't receive share links

## Organization Management

Reference: [Organization Management](https://docs.x.ai/grok/organization)

### What the Admin can do

- View Usage and Billing for the whole organization
- Turn the Personal Workspace on or off for members
- Set Privacy and Data Retention policies
- Manage organization-level API Keys

### Personal Workspace

If a member doesn't see a Personal Workspace, it means the organization's Admin turned this feature off. You can contact xAI Sales to enable it on the Enterprise plan.

## FAQ — Team Management

Reference: [FAQ - Team Management](https://docs.x.ai/grok/faq/team-management)

### Frequently asked questions about team management

**Q: What should I do if a member leaves the organization?**
A: Revoke the License from the Console immediately to prevent access to the Team Workspace.

**Q: Can the Admin see members' conversations?**
A: It depends on the Plan and the Enterprise Tier's Privacy Policy.

**Q: How many people can share a Workspace?**
A: It depends on how many Licenses you bought — there's no fixed limit.

**Q: Can members see each other's Conversations?**
A: No — each person sees only their own Conversation, unless it's shared with the Share button.

## Billing & Subscriptions

Reference: [FAQ - Billing](https://docs.x.ai/grok/faq#billing--subscriptions)

### SuperGrok Subscription

| Sign-up channel | How to manage |
|---|---|
| **Web (grok.com)** | Go to [grok.com/?_s=billing](https://grok.com/?_s=billing) |
| **iOS App Store** | Manage via Apple — [cancel](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428) |
| **Android Google Play** | Manage via Google — [cancel](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/7018481) |

### API Credits

- API Credits **are non-refundable**
- Check Usage at [console.x.ai/team/default/billing](https://console.x.ai/team/default/billing)

### Important notes

- A Subscription is tied to the account that signed up; if you log in with a different account you won't see the Subscription
- **SuperGrok Heavy** has a high Yearly price — check your history before filing a Dispute

## Accounts & Login

Reference: [FAQ - Accounts](https://docs.x.ai/grok/faq#accounts--login)

### Link your X (Twitter) account with xAI

1. On Grok.com go to **Settings → Account**
2. Click **"Connect your X Account"**
3. Do the X SSO Login
4. xAI pulls your X Subscription status and grants the related permissions

Manage your login methods at [accounts.x.ai](https://accounts.x.ai)

### Change your login email

Add or change your email at [accounts.x.ai](https://accounts.x.ai)

### Delete account

Delete your account at [accounts.x.ai/account](https://accounts.x.ai/account). A deleted account can be recovered within **30 days** by logging back in.

### Hacked account

If your account is hacked and the hacker changes the email, contact Support immediately — Support can suspend the Subscription or delete the account for you if you can't verify your identity by email.
