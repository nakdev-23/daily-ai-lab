# Frame Interpolation — make videos smooth

> **Frame Interpolation** (creating in-between frames — making a video smoother) is an AI technique that creates new image frames between existing frames to increase the smoothness of motion.

---

## What is Frame Interpolation?

A normal video has a standard **Frame Rate** (frames per second), e.g.:
- **24fps** (24 frames per second) — the film standard
- **30fps** — the general video standard
- **60fps** — very smooth video

**Frame Interpolation** uses AI to analyze existing frames, then create new frames in between, making the video smoother without reshooting.

**Example:**
- A 24fps video → add in-between frames → 48fps or 60fps
- A choppy slow-motion video → after Interpolation → smooth motion

---

## When to use Frame Interpolation?

### Use when:
- An AI-created video has choppy motion
- You want to make slow motion from a low frame-rate video
- Upgrade an old video to look modern and smoother
- Create a Cinematic smooth video for film or advertising

### Don't use when:
- The video has frequent cuts (Jump cuts) — Interpolation may look strange
- You want to keep the Film look at 24fps (some people like the slight "choppiness" of film)
- The video has lots of text — Interpolation may blur the text

## Understanding Frame Rate

### Frame Rate values and uses

| Frame Rate | Use |
|---|---|
| **24fps** | The film standard, gives a Cinematic feel |
| **25fps** | The PAL standard (Europe) |
| **30fps** | General video, YouTube, News |
| **60fps** | Video games, Sports, Reality Shows |
| **120fps** | Slow motion, VR |

### Why does 24fps look "cinematic"?

The human eye-brain is used to 24fps as "film" because films have been shot at 24fps for 100 years. 60fps video looks like "Reality TV" or "shot live," which some people see as not looking premium.

---

## Slow Motion and Frame Interpolation

**Slow Motion** can be created two ways:

### Method 1: Shoot a High Frame Rate video then play it slow
- Shoot 120fps then play at 24fps = 5x slow motion
- Best quality, but needs a capable camera

### Method 2: Frame Interpolation (AI fills in frames)
- Shoot normal 24fps
- Use AI to add in-between frames, then play slow
- Very good quality for consistent motion
- May have slight distortion in fast motion

