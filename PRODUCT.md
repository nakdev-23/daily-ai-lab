# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Thai learners aged 18–35 who want to add practical AI skills (ChatGPT, Claude, Midjourney, Suno, Runway, Perplexity) to their daily routine. They use the app on mobile and desktop, typically for 10–15 minutes per day. Motivation is habit-driven: streaks, XP, and a quest map keep them returning. Most are non-technical — they want to apply AI at work or in daily life, not study theory.

## Product Purpose

Daily AI Lab teaches AI tools through short, game-like lessons with a mascot (Riri, a cockatiel). Success looks like: users completing their daily goal every day, building a streak, and advancing through topic-based quest maps. The product competes on engagement and delight, not depth.

## Brand Personality

Playful, encouraging, bright. Think: a smart friend who makes learning feel like a game rather than homework. The mascot is central to the emotional register — Riri reacts, celebrates, and guides.

Anti-references: dry e-learning (Coursera aesthetic), clinical SaaS dashboards, anything that feels corporate or intimidating.

## Anti-references

- Coursera / Udemy — utilitarian and dry
- Linear / Notion — too monochrome and "pro tool"
- Corporate EdTech with stock photography
- Dark-mode-only tools that feel like dev environments

## Design Principles

1. **The mascot earns its keep** — Riri should feel alive. Static images are a failure state; she should respond to what the user is doing.
2. **Delight at the right moment** — celebrate completions and streaks; don't animate for decoration between those beats.
3. **Clarity first, then charm** — readable Thai text, accessible contrast, legible at small sizes. Never sacrifice legibility for style.
4. **Game feel, not game noise** — micro-interactions give feedback without overwhelming. One well-placed animation beats ten scattered ones.
5. **Progress is visceral** — XP bars, road fill, ring fill should animate so users *feel* progress moving, not just see a number.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Thai script requires careful line-height (Anuphan at 1.6+). All animations must respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Color is never the sole indicator of state — always pair with shape or label.
