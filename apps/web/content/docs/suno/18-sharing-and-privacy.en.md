# Sharing songs and privacy settings

Once you've created a song, you can control who sees and hears it. This section explains everything about sharing and privacy.

---

## Public vs Private

| Setting | Meaning | Who sees it |
|----------|---------|-------|
| **Public** | the song is visible to everyone | everyone, may appear on Trending |
| **Private** | the song is hidden from the public | only you (and those with the link) |

> **Important:** The **Free** plan always creates songs as Public; you can't set them to Private. If you want Private, you must upgrade to the **Pro** or **Premier** plan.

---

## How to set Public/Private

### While creating the song

1. On the Create Page, before pressing Create, there's a **"Public" / "Private"** option
2. Choose as you wish
3. Create the song

### After creating the song

1. Open the song you want to change
2. Click **"..."** (three dots) and choose **"Edit"** or **"Visibility"**
3. Toggle between Public/Private
4. Save

---

## When does a song appear on Suno's home page?

### Conditions for a song to hit the Home Page or Trending

1. The song must be **Public**
2. The song gets many **Likes** or **Plays**
3. The song is shared on Social Media
4. Suno's Algorithm selects it

### A song set to Private will:
- Not appear on the home page
- Not appear on Explore
- Not be in Search Results
- Still be accessible via a Direct Link

> **Tip:** Even if a song is Private, those with the direct link can still access it. For complete privacy, don't share the link.

## How to share a song

### Share via a link

1. Open the song you want to share
2. Click the **"Share"** or **"Copy Link"** button
3. The link is copied to the Clipboard
4. Paste the link where you want

### Share to Social Media

1. Click **"Share"** and choose the platform:
   - **X (Twitter)** — share with the song embedded
   - **Facebook** — post the link
   - **Instagram** — copy the link into the Bio or Story
   - **TikTok** — use the Hooks feature
2. The song shows a Player when the link is opened

### Download and Upload yourself

If you want to upload the song to another Platform:

1. Download the song as MP3 or MP4
2. Upload it yourself to the Platform you want, such as YouTube, Spotify via a Distributor

## Workspaces

**Workspaces** (a system for managing songs and projects) is a feature that helps organize your songs.

### Benefits of Workspaces

- Create Folders separated by project or Client
- Find songs more easily when you have many
- Share a Workspace with a Collaborator

### How to create a Workspace

1. Go to **Library**
2. Click **"New Workspace"** or **"+"**
3. Name the Workspace
4. Move songs into the Workspace

---

## Deleting songs

### How to delete a song

1. Open the song you want to delete
2. Click **"..."** and choose **"Delete"** or **"Move to Trash"**
3. Confirm the deletion

### Trash

- A deleted song goes into the **Trash** first
- You can Restore a song from the Trash
- The Trash clears automatically after a certain period (generally 30 days)

---

## Privacy of Comments and Interactions

### Turn off Comments on your songs

1. Go to the song you want
2. Click **Edit** or **Settings**
3. Turn off **"Allow Comments"**

### Delete an unwanted Comment

1. Go to that Comment
2. Click **Delete** or **Remove**

> Only the song owner can delete Comments on their own song.

## Downloading songs

### Downloadable formats

| Format | Supported plans | Size | Use |
|-------|----------|-----|-------|
| **MP3** | Free, Pro, Premier | ~5–10 MB | Streaming, Social Media |
| **MP4 (video)** | Free, Pro, Premier | ~30–80 MB | YouTube, Social Media |
| **WAV** | Premier only | ~50–200 MB | Production, Mastering |

### How to download

1. Open the song you want
2. Click **"Download"** or the down-arrow button ↓
3. Choose the format (MP3/MP4/WAV)
4. Wait for the download to finish

---

## Privacy tips

> **Tip:** If you want to test a song before publishing, create it as Private first, then change to Public when ready.

> **Tip:** A song shared as Link Only is still accessible via the link, even if set to Private.

> **Tip:** For Client or Commercial work, always set it to Private until the work is Approved.
