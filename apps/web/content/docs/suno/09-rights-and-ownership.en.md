# Rights and song ownership

When creating songs with AI, the key questions are "Who owns this song?" and "What can I do with it?" This section explains all the rights you have to songs created with Suno.

---

## Who owns songs created with Suno?

### The short answer:

- **Suno doesn't own your songs**
- **You** (the user) have rights to the songs per the terms of the plan you use

### Details by plan

| Plan | Ownership | Rights |
|-----|--------------|-------|
| **Free** | You have personal rights | Non-commercial only |
| **Pro** | You have rights + Commercial | Can use commercially |
| **Premier** | You have rights + full Commercial | Can use commercially fully |

---

## Commercial Use

**Commercial Use** (using songs to generate income or in a business) means:

### Examples that qualify as Commercial Use

- Selling songs or Licensing songs to others
- Putting songs in product/service ads
- Using as Background Music in a monetized YouTube video
- Putting songs in a podcast with a Sponsor
- Using in commercial games, apps, or software
- Playing songs in a store, restaurant, or public place

### Examples that don't qualify as Commercial Use (Non-commercial)

- Listening for personal entertainment
- Sharing with friends without income
- Using in a personal, non-profit project
- Presenting at a university/school (not resold)

---

## Non-commercial Use

**Non-commercial Use** means usage with no money exchanged, no business promotion, and no direct or indirect financial benefit.

**Free Plan** users can only use songs in Non-commercial contexts.

---

## Copyright

**Copyright** (the legal right that protects creative works) is complex when it comes to AI-created songs.

### Status in the United States

Currently, the **US Copyright Office** has a policy that:

- Works created **entirely** by AI without human involvement **cannot be copyrighted**
- Works with significant **human involvement** (such as writing the lyrics yourself) **may be copyrightable**

### Suno's advice

Suno states clearly that "Suno cannot guarantee that the copyright office in your area will grant protection to AI-created songs."

---

## Copyright registration

**Can I register my songs with the copyright office?**

You can **try to register** copyright, but you must state that it's "created with AI," and:

- If there are lyrics you wrote yourself — that lyric portion may be protected
- If the melody was created entirely by AI — the melody portion may not be protected

---

## Distribution on Streaming Platforms

**Can I upload Suno songs to Spotify, Apple Music?**

| Plan | Distribution rights |
|-----|-------------|
| Free | No (no Commercial Use) |
| Pro | Yes |
| Premier | Yes |

### Distribution steps

1. Create the song on the Pro or Premier plan
2. Download the MP3 file (or WAV for Premier)
3. Use a **Music Distributor** service such as:
   - DistroKid
   - TuneCore
   - CD Baby
   - Amuse
4. Upload through the Distributor to Streaming Platforms

> **Important:** You must state that the song was created with AI, per each platform's policy.

---

## Rights for Remix and Extension

### Who owns a Remix?

When you Remix another user's song:

- **The original song** still belongs to the original creator
- **The Remix you create** is yours but with limited rights, since it references the original song
- **Don't profit from the Remix** without permission from the original creator

### Who owns an Extension?

An **Extension** you create from your own song = still your rights.

---

## Do songs made before signing up for Pro get Commercial rights?

**Answer: No**

Commercial rights apply only to songs created **while** you have the Pro/Premier plan. Songs created on the free plan beforehand still have only Non-commercial rights.

---

## When someone else writes the Lyrics in your song

If your song has lyrics written by someone else:

- **The lyric writer** may have rights to that lyric portion
- You should make an Agreement with the writer before using the song commercially
- If you hire them to write, clearly agree on rights (Work-for-hire)
