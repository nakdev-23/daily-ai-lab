# Community Guidelines — usage rules

> Main reference: [Community Guidelines](https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/32013696484109-Community-Guidelines)

---

## Overview

Midjourney has community rules designed to keep the platform creative and safe for everyone. Violating the rules may lead to account suspension or termination.

---

## What's prohibited from creation

### Adult Content
- Don't create images with explicit sexual content
- In a Public Server — creating NSFW content is strictly prohibited
- Even in a Private room there are limitations

### Violent content
- Don't create images showing violence at an inappropriate level
- No overly explicit torture or harm images

### Images of real people
- Beware of creating images of real people in contexts that may be defamatory
- Don't create images that slander or distort the truth
- Beware of Deepfake images (synthetic images with someone else's face)

### Illegal content
- Don't create content illegal in your own country
- Don't create images that violate others' rights

---

## Banned Content (strictly prohibited content)

Midjourney blocks and doesn't allow:
- Content related to child abuse
- Content promoting terrorism
- Content creating hatred based on race, religion, gender

---

## Rules in the Discord community

### Mutual respect
- Don't attack, demean, or harass other members
- No Spam (sending repeated messages with no benefit)

### Appropriateness in public spaces
- Work in a public Server must be appropriate for all ages
- Don't share inappropriate content in public rooms

---

## Commercial-use policy

### Paid users
- Can use commercially (sell, use in work, advertise)
- See the details in the Terms of Service

### Free users (if any)
- Created images are under a Creative Commons License
- Must include Attribution

---

## Reporting violations

If you find content violating the rules:
1. Right-click the message or image
2. Choose "Report"
3. Choose the violation type
4. Submit the report

---

## Consequences of violations

| Level | Result |
|------|-----|
| First warning | a warning |
| Repeat violation | temporary suspension |
| Severe violation | permanent account termination |
| Legal violation | report to authorities |

---

## Basic principles

Midjourney holds to the principles:
- **Be kind** — treat each other with respect
- **Be creative** — use the tool to create
- **Be responsible** — be responsible for what you create

---

## Summary

Use Midjourney to create good and appropriate work. Avoid content that is illegal, obscene, violent, or violates others' rights. Using it responsibly helps the community be a place where everyone can create comfortably.
