# Upscalers — increase image quality

> Main reference: [Upscalers](https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/32804058614669-Upscalers)

---

## What is Upscale

Upscale (the process of increasing an image's resolution from small to large, adding back lost detail) is a post-creation step that increases an image's resolution and detail.

The initial image Midjourney creates has a resolution of about **1024x1024 pixels**; Upscale increases the resolution higher.

---

## Types of Upscale

### Upscale (Subtle)
- Increases resolution about **2x**
- Keeps the original style and Aesthetic
- Adds slightly more detail
- **Good for:** images you're already satisfied with that you just want larger

### Upscale (Creative)
- Increases resolution about **2x**
- Midjourney adds new creative detail
- May change some details slightly for beauty
- **Good for:** when you want the image to be "more complete," or work needing high detail

---

## How to use

### On the website
1. Click the image you want to Upscale
2. Choose **"Upscale (Subtle)"** or **"Upscale (Creative)"**
3. Wait for the result

### On Discord
After creating the 4 images:
- Press **U1, U2, U3, U4** to Upscale image 1, 2, 3, 4

---

## Redo Upscale

If the Upscale Creative result isn't to your liking, you can Upscale again:
- Click **"Redo Upscale (Subtle)"** or **"Redo Upscale (Creative)"**
- Each time may give slightly different detail

---

## Limitations and things to know

- **Uses GPU Time** — Upscale counts GPU Time too (but less than creating a new image)
- **Not Lossless** — Upscale Creative may change some detail
- **Maximum size** — depends on the plan and version

---

## Resolution after Upscale

| Version | Before Upscale | After Upscale |
|---------|-------------|--------------|
| V6.x | ~1024x1024 (1:1) | ~2048x2048 |
| V7 | ~1024x1024 (1:1) | ~2048x2048 |

For other Aspect Ratios, the size adjusts proportionally.

---

## When to Upscale

1. **Before downloading** — if you'll print or actually use it
2. **After choosing the image you're satisfied with** — don't Upscale every image, it wastes GPU Time
3. **For Final Delivery** — the last step before delivering the work

---

## Comparing Upscale Subtle vs Creative

| | Subtle | Creative |
|--|--------|---------|
| Increase resolution | ✅ 2x | ✅ 2x |
| Keep the original style | ✅ a lot | partly |
| Add new detail | ❌ little | ✅ a lot |
| Good for | images you're satisfied with | images needing more Polish |

---

## Tips for using Upscalers

1. **Choose the image before Upscaling** — don't Upscale every image, it wastes GPU Time
2. **Try Subtle first** — if you get the detail you want, no need for Creative
3. **Creative for Illustration** — drawn work often benefits from Creative more than photos
4. **Check the detail after Upscaling** — Upscale Creative may change some detail

---

## Upscale and print work

For print work:
- **Business cards, Flyers** — Upscale Subtle is enough
- **A3 posters** — may need an external AI Upscaler additionally
- **Large banners** — need a Third-party Upscaler such as Topaz

---

## Downloading after Upscale

After Upscaling, click **Download** to save the file:
- The file is a high-quality **PNG**
- For the web, convert to JPG or WebP to save size

---

## Summary

Upscale Subtle is good for enlarging an image while keeping the original style, while Upscale Creative adds new detail to make the image more complete. Choose per your purpose, and Upscale only the images you'll actually use to save GPU Time.
