# 02 — Getting started with Gemini

Reference: [Use Gemini Apps](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13275745) | [Sign In](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13278668) | [Recent Chats](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13666746)

---

## What you need before using it

### Accounts that work with Gemini

You need a Google account, at least one of these:

| Account type | Condition |
|---|---|
| **Personal Google Account** | Age 13+ (or the age set in your country) |
| **Google Workspace account (work)** | Age 18+, and the organization must enable access |
| **School account** | The educational institution must enable it for the administrator |

### Supported browsers (for the web app)

Gemini supports these browsers: **Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Opera, Edge**

> **Note:** Family Link accounts (for children under 13) can't use Gemini. Parents can configure this in Google Family Link.

---

## How to sign in to Gemini (web)

**Steps:**
1. Open a browser and go to **gemini.google.com**
2. Click the **Sign in** button at the top right
3. Sign in with your Google account

**How to sign out:**
1. Go to gemini.google.com
2. Click the **menu (☰)** icon on the left → click your profile picture or initials
3. Choose **Sign out**

---

## Starting a conversation with Gemini

### Start a new chat

1. Go to [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com)
2. Type a question or prompt in the text box at the bottom
3. Click the **Submit** button (the send icon) or press Enter

**Add files or images in the chat:**
- Click the **Add files** icon (📎) and choose a file from your device or Google Drive

**Start a new chat:**
- Click **New chat** (✏️) at the top left

---

## Temporary Chat

**Temporary Chat** is a chat that won't be saved in your history. Use it when you want more privacy.

### What you need
- You must be logged in with a personal account (not a work/school account)
- Available only on the web app and the Gemini mobile app

### How to start a Temporary Chat
1. Go to gemini.google.com
2. Click the **Temporary chat** icon (🕐) next to the New Chat button
3. Just type your question

### Limits of Temporary Chat
- You can't use **Gems**
- You can't connect some **Connected Apps**
- No personalization
- You can't give feedback on the answers

---

## Editing prompts and reviewing answers

### Edit a sent prompt
1. Click the **Edit** icon (✏️) next to your prompt
2. Edit the text
3. Click **Update** — Gemini generates a new answer

### View other draft answers
Gemini sometimes generates several drafts to choose from:
- Click **View other drafts** or **Show drafts** above the answer
- Choose the draft you want

### Create files from the chat
You can ask Gemini to create files directly from the chat, e.g.:

```
"Research the Cavalier King Charles Spaniel and put it in a Google Doc"

"Create a spreadsheet tracking students' weekly math scores"
```

**Supported file types:**
- Google Docs and Google Sheets
- PDF, DOCX, XLSX
- CSV, LaTeX, TXT, RTF, Markdown

---

## Managing chat history

### Find old chats
1. Click the menu (☰) icon on the left
2. View the list of recent chats in the **Recent** section

### Delete a chat
1. Find the chat to delete in Recent
2. Click the 3 dots (⋮) next to that chat
3. Choose **Delete**

> **Note:** If you turn off Keep Activity, chat history won't be saved.

---

## Using Gemini without signing in

On some devices, you can use Gemini's basic features without signing in, e.g.:
- Asking general questions
- Generating simple text

**Features unavailable when not signed in:**
- Chat history
- Personalization
- Connected Apps (Gmail, Drive, Photos, etc.)
- Creating images
- Using Gems
- Uploading files

---

## Location settings

Gemini can use your location to give answers that fit the place better.

**How to check whether Gemini is using your location:**
1. Go to gemini.google.com
2. Click the menu (☰) → **Settings & help**
3. Look at the dot at the bottom — a **blue dot** = using precise location, a **gray dot** = not using it

---

## Can't access it

| Error message | Cause |
|---|---|
| "Can't access this service" | A Family Link account, under the age limit, or an unsupported country |
| "Something went wrong" | A temporary problem; try again later |

---

## Summary

Getting started with Gemini is easy — just have a Google account and go to gemini.google.com and you're ready. For advanced features like Deep Research, Gems, or Connected Apps, you must sign in, and some features require a Google AI Plus/Pro/Ultra plan.
