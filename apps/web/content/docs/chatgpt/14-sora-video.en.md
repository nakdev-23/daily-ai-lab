# Sora — create videos with AI

> Primary reference: [OpenAI Help Center — ChatGPT](https://help.openai.com/en/collections/3742473-chatgpt)

---

## What is Sora

**Sora** is OpenAI's Text-to-Video model (turning text into video) (a trained AI program) that creates AI video from a text prompt (a description typed to instruct the AI), integrated into ChatGPT so users can **create videos right from a ChatGPT conversation**.

Sora uses a **Diffusion Transformer** architecture (an AI structure combining the Diffusion image-creation method with Transformer — making videos more realistic and continuous than before), which lets it create videos that are realistic, continuous, and obey the laws of physics better than previous models.

---

## Sora's abilities

### Text-to-Video
Create videos from a text description:

**Example prompts:**
> *"A 15-second video of a flock of birds flying across an evening sky in golden-orange, cinematic style (movie style)"*

> *"A timelapse (continuous photography sped up greatly — e.g. shooting every minute and playing 24 frames per second) of flowers blooming in a garden at dawn, soft light"*

### Standout realism

**Physics Simulation (the AI understands how objects should move in the real world)**
Sora can "simulate the laws of physics," e.g.:
- The fluidity of water and liquids
- The movement of cloth or hair
- Impacts and gravity
- Light and shadow changing with the camera angle

**Character Consistency (faces and shapes don't change throughout the clip)**
Characters in the video keep their appearance continuously throughout the clip, even when the camera changes angle.

**Dynamic Camera Angles**
It learns to create 3D graphics (3D images made with a computer) and varied camera movement without you specifying the details.

### Audio Integration
- Adds sound effects automatically based on the video content
- Syncs (aligns so sound and image happen together) audio and video naturally

---

## Special features on the Sora platform

### Cameo
A tool that lets you **put your own face into a video** to create personalized content.

### Story Extension (extend a video — continue the story from where the video ends)
- Create an original video and ask Sora to "continue" from where it ends
- Good for creating a story sequence or video series

### Re-cut and Remix (editing and adapting an already-created video)
Modify a video you've created, e.g. change the weather, time of day, or visual style.

---

## How to access Sora through ChatGPT

### Since 2025
OpenAI integrated Sora into ChatGPT, which means you can:
1. Start a regular chat
2. Say you want to create a video
3. ChatGPT sends the prompt to Sora and shows the result back

### Direct access
Go to **sora.com** to use Sora directly, not through the ChatGPT interface (the program's look and operation).

## Metadata and transparency

Every video created with Sora has:

**C2PA Metadata (Content Credentials — data embedded in the file indicating it was made with AI and by whom)**
- Records in the file that it was made by AI
- A standard from the Coalition for Content Provenance and Authenticity (an international standards group for verifying content origin)
- Helps recipients know which content was made with AI

**Visible Watermark (a visible mark — text or a logo shown on the video)**
- In some cases there's a visible watermark
- Shows it was made by OpenAI/Sora

## Limits and what Sora can't do

### Technical limits
- **Complex physics**: still has trouble with very complex physics, e.g. several objects colliding at once
- **Causality**: sometimes doesn't understand cause and effect (e.g. a glass shatters but nothing hit it)
- **Left/Right**: may confuse left and right in spatial reasoning
- **Text in the video**: text appearing in the video may be wrong or unreadable

### Unsupported content
- Violent, pornographic, or hateful content
- Videos of real people that could be misleading (deepfakes — faking faces or voices with AI)
- Content that infringes copyright
- Copyrighted music or audio
