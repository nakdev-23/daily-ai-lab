# Video — create video with Midjourney

> Main reference: [Video](https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/37460773864589-Video)

---

## Midjourney's Video feature

Midjourney has added video capabilities, letting users create short video clips directly from the platform.

---

## Main capabilities

### Text-to-Video
- Create a short video from a Prompt, like creating an image
- Specify the motion you want in the video

### Image-to-Video
- Turn an already-created still image into a video
- Midjourney adds motion to the image

---

## How to create a video

### On the website
1. Go to the Create page
2. Choose **Video** mode (if the option is available)
3. Type a Prompt that includes motion
4. Generate

### Prompt for video
Adding motion descriptions makes the video more interesting:
```
a flower blooming in slow motion, timelapse
a waterfall flowing, camera slowly panning right
clouds moving across a mountain peak
```

---

## Video duration

Videos created with Midjourney are usually **2-5 seconds** long, short clips for:
- Social Media Loops
- Background Video
- Animation Reference
- Short Clips

---

## Example Prompts for video

### Nature Motion
```
gentle waves on a beach, sunset, slow motion
leaves falling from a tree in autumn breeze
```

### Cosmic / Abstract
```
stars twinkling in the night sky, time-lapse
colorful aurora borealis dancing in the sky
```

### Portrait Animation
```
portrait of a woman, hair gently blowing in wind
```

---

## Current limitations

- Videos are short (a few seconds)
- The resolution may be lower than still images
- The feature is still under development

---

## Comparison with other AI video tools

| Tool | Strength |
|----------|--------|
| Midjourney Video | beautiful image style per Midjourney |
| Runway ML | longer, more controllable |
| Kling AI | long video, realistic motion |
| Sora (OpenAI) | long video, high quality |

---

## Using Video

### Social Media Loop
- A short 2-5 second video, good for a Loop on Instagram, TikTok
- Set it to Loop automatically to create a Seamless Video

### Background Video
- Use as a background Motion Graphic
- Good for Presentations, Website Banners

### Teaser Content
- Create a Mood Video for a Brand Teaser
- Show the brand's Aesthetic through motion

---

## Prompts for good motion

Specifying the motion clearly helps you get the video you want:

| Description | Resulting motion |
|---------|----------------|
| `camera slowly zooming in` | slowly zoom in |
| `leaves gently swaying in wind` | leaves swaying in the breeze |
| `light dancing on water surface` | light rippling on the water |
| `smoke rising slowly` | smoke rising |
| `time-lapse of clouds` | clouds flowing fast |

---

## Things to know

- **Length** — currently limited to a few seconds
- **Resolution** — may be lower than still images
- **No sound** — videos from Midjourney have no audio
- **Format** — download as MP4 or GIF

---

## The future of Video in Midjourney

Midjourney is continuously developing the video feature, and it's expected to have longer length and higher resolution in the future. Follow the updates on the Blog and Discord Server.

---

## Summary

Midjourney's Video feature is good for creating short clips with a beautiful image style, good for Social Media Loops, Background Video, and Brand Teasers. For large video work or longer length, you may need additional specialized tools such as Runway ML or Kling AI.
