# Publishing — put your app online 🚀

> Compiled in English from the official docs at [docs.lovable.dev](https://docs.lovable.dev/)

When your app is ready, publish it for others to use in just a few clicks.

## 🌐 Publish

1. Click the **Publish** button in your project
2. Lovable deploys the app and gives you a **public link**
3. Share the link for others to use right away

Whenever you edit the app and want the live site updated, just click Publish again.

## 🔗 Custom Domain (your own domain)

Want to use your own domain name (e.g. `myapp.com`) instead of the default link:
1. Go to the domain settings in your project
2. Add the domain you own
3. Configure the DNS as the system instructs
4. Wait for it to connect successfully

## 💡 Tips

- Test important features before Publishing (especially login/data)
- Tighten data access permissions before going public
- A custom domain makes your app look more professional
