# Inpainting and Outpainting — remove and expand images with AI

> **Inpainting** and **Outpainting** are professional-grade image-editing tools using AI — remove unwanted things, or expand an image beyond its original edges.

---

## What is Inpainting?

**Inpainting** (removing and replacing parts of an image — like Photoshop but using AI) is the process of selecting parts of an image and having the AI remove what's there and create new content to replace it.

**Inpainting usage examples:**
- Remove unwanted people from a photo
- Remove a Watermark or unwanted Logo
- Change objects in an image, e.g. change a car from red to blue
- Replace part of the background

---

## What is Outpainting?

**Outpainting** (expanding an image beyond its frame) is the process of extending an image beyond its original edges, with the AI creating content that's reasonable and consistent with the source image.

**Outpainting usage examples:**
- A portrait image → expand to landscape for YouTube
- An image shot too tightly → add space around it
- Change the Aspect Ratio without cropping

---

## How to use Inpainting

### Step 1: Upload an image

1. Go to the **"Inpainting"** or **"Erase & Replace"** tool
2. Upload the image you want to edit

### Step 2: Draw a Mask

A **Mask** (the area you want the AI to change) is the white area you paint over the part you want to remove or change.

Tools used:
- **Brush** — paint by hand, adjustable size
- **Lasso** (drawing a line around an area) — draw a line surrounding the desired area
- **Auto-select** — the AI helps select the area

### Step 3: Write a Prompt (optional)

If you want the AI to replace it with something specific, add a Prompt:

```
Replace with a blue sky and white clouds
```

If you just want to remove it, no Prompt needed — the AI creates a background consistent with the surrounding area.

### Step 4: Generate

The AI fills in the masked area to look complete and blended.

---

## How to use Outpainting

### Step 1: Upload an image

1. Go to **"Outpainting"** or **"Expand Image"**
2. Upload the source image

### Step 2: Choose a new Aspect Ratio

Choose the desired Aspect Ratio:
- From 1:1 → 16:9 (add space on the sides)
- From 9:16 → 1:1 (add space on top-bottom)
- Set a custom size

### Step 3: Set the expansion direction

Choose which way to expand:
- Expand all 4 sides
- Expand only the right side
- Expand only the top
- etc.

### Step 4: Write a Prompt (recommended)

A Prompt helps the AI know what to create in the new area:

```
Continue the forest landscape with more trees and mountains in the distance
```

---

## Techniques for effective use

### Inpainting Tips
1. **Draw the Mask to cover it** — if the Mask is too small, the AI may not remove everything
2. **Add space around the edges** — draw the Mask with a little extra space around the object to remove
3. **Use a Prompt if you want a specific result** — if you don't, the AI creates at its discretion
4. **Try several times** — the result may differ each time

### Outpainting Tips
1. **Start from an image with clear edges** — images with a clear background work better
2. **The Prompt should be consistent with the original** — if expanding a sea image, say you want more sea, not a forest
3. **Expand a little at a time** — expanding a bit at a time then expanding further works better than expanding a lot at once

## Background Removal

**Background Removal** is a feature related to Inpainting that automatically removes the background from an image.

How to use it:
1. Upload an image
2. Choose **"Remove Background"**
3. The AI removes the background, leaving just the main object or person
4. Download as PNG (an image file format supporting a transparent background) with an Alpha Channel (the image's transparency)

**Benefits:**
- Place a person or product image on a new background
- Create a Thumbnail for YouTube
- Do clean Product photography
