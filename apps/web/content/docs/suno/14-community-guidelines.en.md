# Community Guidelines

**Community Guidelines** (the rules everyone in the Suno community must follow) help make Suno a safe, creative, and fair space for everyone.

Based on the [Suno Community Guidelines](https://suno.com/community-guidelines) (last updated: January 2025)

---

## Basic principles

Suno wants the community to be a place that is:

1. **Mutually respectful** — everyone has the right to create freely
2. **Creative** — encourages experimentation and expression
3. **Safe** — free from harassment and harmful content
4. **Honest** — no impersonation or false information

---

## Content prohibited from creation

### 1. Inappropriate sexual content

- Adult Content unsuitable for users under 13
- Content involving children inappropriately (illegal)

### 2. Violence and terrorism

- Songs that support or promote Terrorism
- Content that glorifies violence against people or groups
- Content calling for harm to others

### 3. Discriminatory content and Hate Speech

**Hate Speech** includes content that:
- Discriminates based on Race, Nationality, religion, Gender, Sexual Orientation
- Demeans or promotes hatred toward any group of people

### 4. Misinformation

- Songs that spread false information about real events
- Impersonating a real person
- Damaging Deepfakes

### 5. Illegal content

- Promoting drugs, weapons, or illegal activities
- Content that violates others' Privacy

---

## Intellectual Property rules

**Intellectual Property** (creative work that is legally protected) includes:

### What you shouldn't do

- **Copying copyrighted lyrics** without permission
- **Impersonating a famous artist** to deceive
- **Using others' song titles or Trademarks** without permission

### Rules on Copyright and AI

Suno partners with **Audible Magic** (an audio copyright-checking company) to:

- Verify that created songs don't infringe existing song copyrights
- Filter content that duplicates Copyrighted songs

---

## Usage rules and Self-Promotion

### Self-Promotion

- **Allowed** — share your songs in the community space
- **Not allowed** — Spam (sending repeated messages excessively) to gain Followers
- **Not allowed** — buying or selling Engagement (fake Likes, Comments, Shares)
- **Not allowed** — using Bots to boost play counts

### Privacy

- **Don't disclose** others' personal information without permission
- **Don't upload** others' voices without permission
- **Don't impersonate** another person in the community

---

## Giving Feedback and Comments

### Good practices

- Give constructive Feedback — focus on the content, not the creator
- Show appreciation when a song is good
- Ask questions politely

### What you shouldn't do

- **Harassment** — repeatedly hurting others emotionally
- **Bullying** — demeaning or mocking work
- **Trolling** — commenting to deliberately create conflict

---

## Reporting inappropriate content

### Report a Comment

1. Click the Comment you want to report
2. Click **"Report"**
3. Choose a reason (Harassment, Spam, Inappropriate, etc.)
4. Submit the report

### Report a Song

1. Open the song you want to report
2. Click **"..."** and choose **"Report"**
3. Choose the appropriate reason
4. Submit the report

### Turn off Comments on your songs

1. Open your song
2. Click **Settings** or **Edit**
3. Turn off **"Allow Comments"**

### Delete a Comment on your songs

1. Open your song
2. Find the Comment you want to delete
3. Click **"Delete"** or **"Remove"**

---

## The Moderation system

**Suno uses an automated Moderation system** that:

- Scans the lyrics and Style of every Request
- Automatically blocks Prompts that violate the Guidelines
- Has the team review reported songs

### Consequences of violations

| Violation level | Action |
|--------------|------------|
| Mild | Warning |
| Moderate | Temporary Suspension |
| Severe | Permanent Termination |
| Illegal | Notify the relevant authorities |

## CA AB 2013 Disclosure

**CA AB 2013** is a law of the state of California, USA, requiring AI companies to disclose information about:

- The Training Data used to train the AI
- How content is created
- The risks of usage

Suno complies with this law and publishes the information on its website as the law requires.

---

## Minimum Age

- Users must be at least **13 years** old
- Users aged 13–17 must have parental consent
- Suno reserves the right to suspend accounts suspected of being underage children
