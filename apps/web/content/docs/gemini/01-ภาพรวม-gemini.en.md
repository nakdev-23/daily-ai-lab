# 01 — Overview: what is Gemini?

Reference: [Use Gemini Apps](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13275745) | [Gemini Overview](https://gemini.google/overview/)

---

## What is Gemini

Gemini is Google's AI assistant, built to help you work more easily, whether writing, planning, learning, or researching.

Gemini runs on Large Language Model (LLM) technology, an AI system trained on a vast amount of text so it understands human language naturally.

---

## What can you do with Gemini

Gemini helps you with many things, including:

**Writing**
- Write emails, blog posts, poems, stories
- Help draft initial content, then refine it as you want
- Translate languages or adjust the writing tone to formal/informal

**Planning and brainstorming**
- Help brainstorm new ideas
- Plan projects, trips, or studies
- Propose options for solving various problems

**Research and learning**
- Summarize complex topics simply
- Answer questions, explain concepts
- Help analyze data, documents, or code
- Do Deep Research across many sources at once

**Creativity**
- Create images, videos, or music with AI
- Create presentation slides or documents
- Build simple apps or web pages through Canvas

**Task management**
- Connect to Google Calendar, Gmail, Drive
- Help manage email, schedule work
- Use the Gemini Agent to do multi-step work for you

## Example questions you can use

Here are examples from the official docs to show what Gemini can do:

```
"Help me make an outline for a blog post about summer drink recipes"

"Debug this error for me: FileNotFoundError: No such file or directory"

"I want to learn Python — where should I start? Make me a study plan and suggest videos"

"I like playing games and drawing — what jobs might I do when I grow up?"
```

---

## Cautions before using

Gemini is a powerful AI, but it can make mistakes. Remember:

- **Always verify information** — don't trust answers 100% without further checking
- **Don't use it in place of an expert** — don't use Gemini's advice in place of advice from a doctor, lawyer, or financial advisor
- **Be careful with personal data** — don't put passwords, credit card numbers, or sensitive data in the chat

---

## Summary

Gemini is Google's all-in-one AI assistant, usable in many forms — from text conversation, talking by voice, and creating images to deep research and doing multi-step work for you. It's good for both beginners and those who want advanced use.
