# Voices and Custom Models in v5.5

Suno v5.5 (March 2026) introduces features that bring a major change in **Personalization** with three main features: **Voices**, **Custom Models**, and **My Taste**.

---

## Voices — use your voice in Suno

**Voices** (a feature that lets the AI learn and use your vocal characteristics) is one of the most important innovations of Suno v5.5.

### What can Voices do?

- **Record your voice** and let the AI learn its characteristics
- Create songs the AI sings with a **Vocal Character** inspired by your voice
- Reuse a created Voice across different songs

### How to create a Voice

1. Go to **Profile > Voices** or **Create > Voices**
2. Click **"Create New Voice"**
3. **Record voice samples:**
   - Read the given text (about 30–60 seconds)
   - Sing a short song so the AI captures the vocal characteristics
   - Suno needs samples of several types
4. Wait for the AI to process the Voice (may take 2–5 minutes)
5. Name the Voice and save

### Using a created Voice

1. On the Create page, choose **"My Voices"**
2. Choose the Voice you want
3. Create a song as usual — the AI uses the Vocal Character from the chosen Voice

---

## Voices FAQ

### Will the created voice exactly match my real voice?

Not necessarily — Suno doesn't clone your voice but creates a Vocal Character **inspired** by your voice. The result is similar but not 100% identical.

### Who can use the Voice I create?

Only **you** (unless you share it).

### Can I create a Voice from someone else's voice?

**No** — Suno has a verification system to prevent using others' voices without permission. Attempting this is considered a violation of the Terms of Service.

### Voice privacy

- Uploaded voices are **Encrypted** and stored securely
- Suno doesn't share your voice data with third parties
- You can delete a Voice at any time

## Custom Models

**Custom Models** in v5.5 is a feature that lets you "teach" the AI to create songs in your unique style.

### How do Custom Models differ from Personas?

| | **Personas** | **Custom Models** |
|--|------------|-----------------|
| Scope | capture the "Vibe" of one song | trained from several songs |
| Accuracy | medium | higher |
| Creation time | fast (from 1 song) | slower (must feed several songs) |
| Flexibility | high | specialized |

### How to create a Custom Model

1. Go to **Profile > Custom Models**
2. Click **"Create New Model"**
3. **Feed sample songs** (Training Songs), at least 5–10 songs:
   - Upload your own MP3/WAV songs
   - Or choose songs you've created in Suno
4. Name the Model, e.g. "My Dark Jazz Style"
5. Click **"Train Model"** — wait for the AI to learn (may take 10–30 minutes)
6. Test the Model by creating a new song

### When should you create a Custom Model

- You want songs that sound "like me" consistently
- You do professional Music Production and want a Signature Sound
- You create songs for a specific Brand or IP (Intellectual Property)
- You want high Consistency across every song you create

## My Taste

**My Taste** is a **Recommendation Engine** (an AI that learns your preferences) that learns from your Suno usage behavior.

### What does My Taste learn from?

- Songs you **Like**
- Music styles you create often
- Genres you listen to for a long time
- Songs you Play repeatedly
- Style Prompts you use often

### Benefits of My Taste

1. **Personalized Explore** — the Explore page shows songs you're likely to enjoy
2. **Smart Suggestions** — when you start Create, the system suggests Styles matching your preferences
3. **Discover New Artists** — recommends Creators with a similar style

### How to enable My Taste

1. Go to **Settings > Preferences**
2. Turn on the **"My Taste"** toggle
3. Use Suno as usual — the system gradually learns

### Clearing My Taste data

If you want to Reset (start over):
1. Go to **Settings > Preferences > My Taste**
2. Click **"Reset My Taste Data"**

## Important things to know about Voices

### Ethics and consent

Suno holds to the principles:

- **Consent** — don't use others' voices without permission
- **Transparency** — content created with an AI Voice should be labeled as AI
- **Ownership** — you own a Voice created from your own voice

### CA AB 2013 and Voice Cloning

The California AB 2013 law that Suno complies with includes a part about Voice Cloning, prohibiting creating a fake voice of a real person without permission.

## Tips

> **Tip:** Record in a quiet place with no background noise, so the Custom Voice has the highest quality.

> **Tip:** Try using a Voice together with a Custom Model to get a fully unique "voice + style" of your own.

> **Tip:** The more you use Suno, the more accurate My Taste becomes — give it 2–3 weeks before the system knows your preferences well.
