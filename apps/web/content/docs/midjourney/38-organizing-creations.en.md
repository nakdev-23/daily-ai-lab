# Organizing your work

> Main references: [Organizing Your Creations](https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/33329462451469-Organizing-Your-Creations), [Using Folders](https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/34580542725645-Using-Folders)

---

## The importance of organizing

As you create more images, organizing helps you:
- Find the image you want quickly
- Group work by project
- Track your favorite work and usable work

---

## Archive — the main gallery

The Archive (where all the images you create are collected) is the place gathering every image you've ever created.

### Searching in the Archive
- Search by **Prompt** — type the words used to create it
- Search by **date** — choose a time range
- Filter by **Like, Saved, Recent**

---

## Folders

Folders (where images are stored by category, like folders on a computer) help organize images by project or topic.

### Create a folder
1. Go to the Archive or the left menu
2. Click **"New Folder"** or **"+"**
3. Name the folder

### Move images into a folder
- Right-click the image → **"Move to Folder"**
- Or drag the image into the folder (Drag and Drop)

### Example folder structure
```
📁 Client Work
   📁 Client A - Logo Project
   📁 Client B - Product Photos
📁 Personal Projects
   📁 Fantasy Illustrations
   📁 Landscape Series
📁 Reference
   📁 Style Experiments
   📁 Prompt Testing
```

---

## Liked Images

- Click ❤️ on any image in Explore to Like it
- See all your Liked images in the **"Liked"** section of the Archive
- Use it as a personal Inspiration source

---

## Moodboards

Moodboards (collecting several images in one place to set the direction and atmosphere of a project) help you plan and communicate a project's Concept.

### Create a Moodboard
1. Go to **"Moodboards"** in the left menu
2. Click **"New Moodboard"**
3. Name it and add images

### Add images to a Moodboard
- From the Archive: right-click → "Add to Moodboard"
- From Explore: click the image → "Save to Moodboard"

### Benefits of a Moodboard
- Plan the Visual Direction before starting work
- Share with the team or client to confirm the Concept
- Combine Reference Images and created images together

---

## Complete Tasks

The Complete Tasks feature (a list of things to do to get free Fast Hours or Unlock features) has Tasks to do to get free Fast Time:

- Rate Images — helps develop Personalization
- Complete Tutorials
- Invite Friends

---

## Tips for organizing

1. **Create folders from the start** — don't wait until you have many images to organize
2. **Name folders clearly** — "Project Name - Date" or "Topic - Style"
3. **Use a Moodboard before creating** — gather References before starting the real Prompt
4. **Like inspiring images** — save them to see the Prompt later

---

## Summary

Good work organization makes your Workflow efficient. Use Folders for projects, Moodboards for Concept Planning, and Liked Images for Inspiration.
