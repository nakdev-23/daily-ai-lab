# Hooks — create short video content from songs

**Hooks** (a feature combining songs with short videos to share on Social Media) is a new Suno feature designed for sharing on Short-form Platforms.

---

## What is Hooks?

Hooks lets you create **Short-form Content** (short videos — 15–60 seconds long) from songs you create in Suno by:

- Choosing the best part of the song (usually the Chorus or Hook)
- Adding Visuals (animation) that fit the music
- Creating a Portrait (vertical) video ready to share immediately

---

## Why use Hooks?

| Reason | Detail |
|-------|---------|
| **Viral Potential** | Short-form content has a higher chance of being shared than long content |
| **Easy and fast** | create a Hook in a few steps |
| **Platform Ready** | the size and format are ready for TikTok, Reels, Shorts |
| **Music Promotion** | promote the full song so people want to listen |

---

## How to create a Hook

### Through the Suno website

1. Open the song you want to make a Hook from
2. Click **"Create Hook"** or the Hooks icon
3. Choose the part of the song you want:
   - Drag the Slider to select the time range
   - Or choose the section you want (Verse, Chorus, Bridge)
4. Choose a Visual Style:
   - **Waveform** — shows a Waveform moving with the sound
   - **Album Art** — shows the song's artwork
   - **Lyrics** — shows the lyrics animated
   - **Abstract Visuals** — AI-generated images based on the song's mood
5. Customize:
   - Color Scheme
   - the font, if showing Lyrics
   - the text size and position
6. Click **"Generate Hook"**
7. Download or share directly

### Through the Mobile app

1. Open the Suno app
2. Go to the song you want
3. Tap the **"Hooks"** icon
4. Follow the same steps
5. Share directly from the app

## Platforms suited to Hooks

| Platform | Suitable size | Recommended length |
|----------|-----------|----------------|
| **TikTok** | 1080 x 1920 (9:16) | 15–60 seconds |
| **Instagram Reels** | 1080 x 1920 (9:16) | 15–90 seconds |
| **YouTube Shorts** | 1080 x 1920 (9:16) | 15–60 seconds |
| **Twitter/X** | 1280 x 720 (16:9) | 15–30 seconds |

> Suno Hooks come out in 9:16 (vertical) format suited to TikTok and Reels.

---

## Techniques for an effective Hook

### Choose the best part

- The **Chorus** is usually the most "catchy" part
- Choose a part with high Energy or distinctiveness
- Avoid a long Intro and a quiet Outro

### Visuals that match the Mood

| Song Mood | Recommended Visual |
|---------|--------------|
| Energetic, EDM | Abstract Visuals, bright colors |
| Sad, Ballad | a plain Waveform, dark tones |
| Storytelling, Folk | Lyrics showing the words |
| Pop, Catchy | Lyrics + bright colors |

## Real-life examples of using Hooks

### For a Music Creator

```
Situation: created a new Pop song in Suno
Steps:
1. Create the song (2 minutes)
2. Create a Hook from the Chorus (30 seconds)
3. Upload to TikTok with an inviting Caption
4. Attach a link to Suno for those who want to hear the whole song
Result: promote the full song through Short-form Content
```

### For a Content Creator

```
Situation: need Background Music that fits a video
Steps:
1. Create an Instrumental in Suno (matching the video Theme)
2. Create a Hook to show a preview
3. Use the full Instrumental in the actual video
Result: have an Original song with no copyright issues
```

## Hooks and copyright

- A Hook created from **your own** Suno song — usable per your plan's rights
- **Free Plan:** can use Hooks but with Commercial Use limitations
- **Pro/Premier:** can use Hooks commercially

## Tips

> **Tip:** Try both 15 seconds and 30 seconds to see which length Engages better.

> **Tip:** Add a Call-to-Action (CTA) in the Caption, e.g. "Listen to the full song at the link in Bio."

> **Tip:** Hooks that show Lyrics communicate very well in environments with no sound, like watching a video in public without headphones.
