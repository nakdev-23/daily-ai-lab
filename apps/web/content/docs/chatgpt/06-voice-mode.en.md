# Voice Mode & Advanced Voice

> Primary reference: [OpenAI Help Center — ChatGPT](https://help.openai.com/en/collections/3742473-chatgpt)

---

## What is Voice Mode

ChatGPT has two levels of voice conversation: regular **Voice Mode** and **Advanced Voice Mode (AVM)**. Both let you talk to ChatGPT directly without typing.

### The difference between the two modes

| Property | Regular Voice Mode | Advanced Voice Mode |
|---|---|---|
| How it works | Converts speech to text, then converts the answer back to speech | The AI processes the audio directly, without conversion |
| Naturalness | Sounds like Text-to-Speech (automatically turning letters into spoken voice) reading | Converses like talking with a real person |
| Emotion awareness | None | Senses the speaker's tone and emotion |
| Interruption | You must wait for it to finish | You can interrupt mid-sentence |
| Pausing | None | You can pause like in a real conversation |

---

## Advanced Voice Mode (AVM)

### Main features

**Real-time conversation (instantaneous — no waiting), no lag**
Advanced Voice Mode processes speech directly, making the delay, or latency (the time the system takes to respond — the lower the better), very low. You speak and hear the answer almost instantly, like a phone call with a very smart assistant.

**Detects emotion and tone**
AVM can "sense and respond to the user's emotion," e.g.:
- If you speak in a worried tone, the AI responds reassuringly
- If you speak cheerfully, the AI responds in an upbeat tone
- If you speak quickly, the AI responds concisely

**Interrupt at any time**
No need to wait for the AI to finish; you can interrupt mid-sentence, just like a natural real conversation.

**Voice Options (the AI's voice choices)**
OpenAI has voices created with professional voice actors, including:
- **Alloy** — a middle, neutral tone, good for general use
- **Echo** — a low voice, professional-sounding
- **Fable** — a warm, friendly-sounding voice
- **Onyx** — a deep, powerful voice
- **Nova** — a higher voice, bright-sounding
- **Shimmer** — a soft, relaxing voice

### Voice safety

- The system **cannot imitate the voice of a real or public figure**
- It has filters preventing the creation of music or copyrighted content
- Every voice is created together with paid professional actors

## How to use Voice Mode

### On the mobile app (iOS & Android)

1. Open the ChatGPT app
2. Tap the **microphone** button at the bottom-right of the screen
3. Wait for the circle to ripple (showing it's ready to listen)
4. Just speak
5. Tap stop, or keep speaking, when you want

### On Advanced Voice Mode

1. Tap the **headphones** icon or choose Voice Mode from the menu
2. The screen changes to a full-screen voice-conversation mode
3. Start speaking right away — the AI listens continuously
4. Tap **X** to exit voice mode

### Extra features in AVM

- **Video & Screen Sharing** (letting the AI see what you see): you can share your screen or open the camera for the AI to see during the conversation (this feature is rolling out to users)
- **Hands-free** (used without holding or touching the device): use it while doing other activities — driving, exercising, cooking

## Recommended use cases

### Language practice
Voice Mode is great for practicing speaking a foreign language because:
- The AI replies in the same language you speak
- You can ask the AI to correct your pronunciation or grammar
- Practice anytime, without embarrassment

### Asking questions while your hands are busy
- Ask while cooking
- Ask for directions while driving (with attention to safety)
- Brainstorm ideas while walking

### Meetings and rehearsals
- Rehearse a presentation (showing your work or explaining to others)
- Practice answering interview questions
- Brainstorm out loud

## Limits and things to know

| Topic | Details |
|---|---|
| **Supported plans** | AVM: Plus, Pro, Team, Enterprise (Free has limited use) |
| **Languages** | Supports over 45 languages, tested by red teamers from 29 countries |
| **Recording** | OpenAI may keep voice recordings to improve the model (the trained AI program); configurable in Privacy |
| **Cross-conversation context** | Voice Mode in each conversation is independent, except when Memory is on |
| **Some commands** | Some complex commands may work better through typing |

## Usage tips

- **Speak clearly and in full sentences**: the AI understands context (the surrounding information that helps make meaning clear) better than fragmented speech
- **Interrupt when you want**: no need to wait for the AI to finish; if the answer is off, just say so
- **Change the voice to match your mood**: try different voices to find one you like, configurable in Settings > Speech
- **Use it with Memory** (a memory system — letting the AI remember your important info across conversations): turn on Memory so the AI remembers your name, preferences, and life context, for a more personal conversation
