# Zoom Out — expand the image's boundaries

> Main reference: [Zoom Out](https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/32595476770957-Zoom-Out)

---

## What is Zoom Out

Zoom Out (zooming out — expanding the image's boundaries outward, with Midjourney creating new content around the source image) lets you "step back" from the image to see a wider context.

It's like pulling the camera back, letting you see a wider scene around the original image.

---

## Zoom Out options

### Zoom Out 2x
- Expands the boundary 2 times
- The original image stays in the center, at half the size of the new image

### Zoom Out 1.5x
- Expands the boundary moderately
- Good when you want to expand just a little

### Custom Zoom
- Enter a value between 1.0 and 2.0 yourself
- e.g. 1.3x or 1.8x

---

## How to use

### On the website
1. Create and Upscale the image first
2. Click **"Zoom Out 2x"** or **"Zoom Out 1.5x"**
3. Wait for the result

### On Discord
After Upscaling:
- Press **"Zoom Out 2x"** or **"Zoom Out 1.5x"** that appears under the image

---

## Usage examples

### Portrait → Full Body
```
Original image: a close-up Portrait of the face
After Zoom Out 2x: see the shoulders, torso, or whole body + background
```

### Close-up → Scene
```
Original image: a Close-up flower
After Zoom Out 2x: see the whole flower garden around that flower
```

### Create a Widescreen from a vertical image
```
Original image: a square Portrait
Zoom Out 2x and change the Aspect Ratio: get a Widescreen image
```

---

## Make Square

The "Make Square" button helps convert a non-1:1 image to a square by expanding the shorter side outward.

---

## Custom Zoom with a Prompt change

In Custom Zoom you can add a Prompt for the new expanded area:

```
Custom Zoom 2x + Prompt: "surrounded by ancient ruins, foggy atmosphere"
```

→ the original image stays in the center, but the new area around it will be ruins in fog

---

## Tips

1. **Upscale before Zoom Out** — gets better quality
2. **Reusable** — Zoom Out repeatedly to expand further and further
3. **Combine with Pan** — expand out, then shift in the direction you want

---

## Zoom Out repeatedly (Infinite Zoom)

Zooming Out repeatedly creates an "Infinite Zoom" or "Zoom to Reveal" effect:

```
First image: a Close-up flower (1:1)
Zoom Out 2x: see the branch and leaves
Zoom Out 2x again: see the whole tree
Zoom Out 2x again: see the whole garden
```

→ good for creating a Video Zoom Effect or Storytelling

---

## Change the Aspect Ratio while Zooming Out

Custom Zoom lets you change the Aspect Ratio along with Zoom Out:
```
Original image: 1:1
Custom Zoom 1.5x → change the Aspect Ratio to 16:9
```
→ get a Widescreen image expanded out at the same time

---

## Things to know

- **Image edges** — the part Zoom Out adds may have different quality from the original
- **Consistency** — Midjourney tries to make the new part consistent with the original, but doesn't guarantee 100%
- **Size limit** — you can't Zoom Out infinitely large; it depends on the maximum supported size

---

## Summary

Zoom Out is a creative tool that lets you expand an image while keeping the original in the center. It's good for creating a wide scene from a Close-up image, adding context, or creating an Infinite Zoom Effect for Video Content. Use Custom Zoom to control the distance and change the Aspect Ratio at the same time.
