# AI Avatars — choose or create your avatar 🧑‍🎤

> Compiled in English from the official docs at [docs.heygen.com](https://docs.heygen.com/), the Avatars section

An avatar is the "AI actor" that speaks your script for you. HeyGen offers both ready-made and self-created ones.

## 🗂️ Avatar types

| Type | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Stock Avatars** | Hundreds of ready-made avatars, ready to use |
| **Instant Avatar** | Your own avatar, created from a short video recording |
| **Photo Avatar** | An avatar from a photo (including AI-generated images) |
| **Studio Avatar** | A high-quality avatar recorded in a studio (for enterprise work) |

## 🎥 Create an Instant Avatar (overview)

1. Go to **Avatars → Create → Instant Avatar**
2. Record a video of yourself per the guidance (look at the camera, good lighting, speak the given script)
3. **Confirm consent** that it's you
4. Wait for the system to process, then use the avatar to speak any script

## 🖼️ Photo Avatar

- Upload a photo of a person (or use an AI-generated image) and make it speak/move
- Great for creating a unique presenter without filming a video

## ⚠️ About consent

Creating an avatar from a real person's face/voice must be **yourself** or with **permission** — HeyGen has an identity-verification system to prevent deepfaking others.
